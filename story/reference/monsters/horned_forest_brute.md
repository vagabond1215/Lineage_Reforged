# Horned Forest Brute

Name: Horned Forest Brute
Classification: Large Beast
Encountered In: Chapter 4
Data Confidence: Medium

## Habitat
- Dense cedar and conifer bands along unstable valley roads.
- Often appears near choke points where mobility is limited.

## Primary Attacks
- Direct charge with tusk impact.
- Body slam and trampling follow-through.
- Roar pressure used to panic weaker party members.

## Special Attacks
- Enraged rush after taking lightning or blade wounds.
- Terrain-breaking momentum (roots, light barriers, camp setups).

## Abilities and Traits
- Extreme mass and short-burst power.
- High pain tolerance during active rage state.
- Durable hide with strong surface wound resistance.

## Elemental Resistances
- High tolerance to blunt trauma.
- Can continue fighting after non-core lightning hits.

## Elemental Weaknesses
- Eye strikes and vision disruption (fire/oil proved effective).
- Under-jaw and throat channel vulnerable to precision blades.

## Byproducts
Materials:
- Heavy hide (shield-facing leather layers).
- Tusks (weapon grips, carving stock).
- Horn core (structural craft pieces).
- Dense sinew (high-tension cordage).

Ingredients:
- Marrow fat (long-burn alchemical base).
- Blood salts (rage-tonic experiments; unsafe without refinement).
