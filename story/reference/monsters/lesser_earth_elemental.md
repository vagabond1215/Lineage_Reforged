# Lesser Earth Elemental

Name: Lesser Earth Elemental
Classification: Minor Elemental Construct
Encountered In: Chapter 5
Data Confidence: High

## Habitat
- Embedded in mine walls, floors, and collapsed side galleries.
- Most active in unstable tunnel zones with old excavation stress.

## Primary Attacks
- Body slam and shoulder impact.
- Jagged limb strike from close range.
- Swarm pressure to pin targets in narrow passages.

## Special Attacks
- Emergence from wall/floor surfaces with minimal warning.
- Sequential wave attacks when chamber resonance is active.

## Abilities and Traits
- Can assemble rapidly from surrounding mineral mass.
- No fear response and no visible fatigue pattern.
- Effective in confined spaces where maneuver is limited.

## Elemental Resistances
- High resistance to slashing and piercing that does not reach the core.
- Immune to morale and intimidation effects.

## Elemental Weaknesses
- Concentrated lightning discharge.
- High-force impact that fractures core structure.
- Repeated strikes to luminous seam lines.

## Byproducts
Materials:
- Core shards (resonant catalyst stock).
- Compacted geomass (reinforced mortar input).
- Mineral plate fragments (abrasive tool stock).

Ingredients:
- Resonant dust (earth-aligned alchemy, stabilizer additive).
- Trace molten grit (forge flux blends, limited use).
