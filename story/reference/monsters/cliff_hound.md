# Cliff Hound

Name: Cliff Hound
Classification: Beast / Pack Predator
Encountered In: Chapter 4
Data Confidence: Medium

## Habitat
- Wet forest shelf roads and ravine crossings in the Western Valley.
- Prefers low-visibility conditions (rain, dusk, fog).

## Primary Attacks
- Leap-and-bite ambush.
- Pack flanking pressure.
- Limb-targeting bite to disable movement.

## Special Attacks
- Ravine shove during narrow trail crossings.
- Coordinated pounce when target formation breaks.

## Abilities and Traits
- Fast acceleration over short distance.
- Effective camouflage in rain-dark terrain.
- Strong jaw clamp once contact is established.

## Elemental Resistances
- None confirmed.

## Elemental Weaknesses
- High-voltage lightning shock (observed lethal response).
- Vulnerable throat and neck region.

## Byproducts
Materials:
- Hide strips (light armor reinforcement, strapwork).
- Fangs (dagger inlay, trap points).
- Claw tips (needle tools).
- Tendon (binding cord).

Ingredients:
- Adrenal tissue (stimulant brew base, low stability).
- Blood (tracking compounds, ritual scenting).
