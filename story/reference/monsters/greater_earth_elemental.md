# Greater Earth Elemental

Name: Greater Earth Elemental
Classification: Major Elemental Construct
Encountered In: Chapter 5
Data Confidence: Medium

## Habitat
- Deep mine chambers near major structural stress lines.
- Appears tied to active fault pressure or disturbed core zones.

## Primary Attacks
- Overhead double-fist crush strikes.
- Heavy forward pressure and chamber denial.
- Step impact that fractures footing and positioning.

## Special Attacks
- Ground-heave emergence from beneath central chamber floor.
- Sustained pressure role while lesser elementals flank targets.

## Abilities and Traits
- Layered rock armor plates with shifting lock pattern.
- Molten core signatures visible through chest seams.
- High durability under mixed-attack conditions.

## Elemental Resistances
- Very high resistance to conventional physical damage.
- Moderate tolerance to lightning unless focused on seams/core.

## Elemental Weaknesses
- Joint disruption (knee/hinge seams).
- Core seam penetration under repeated force.
- Combined stagger plus focused follow-up on exposed fractures.

## Byproducts
Materials:
- Greater core fragment (high-grade catalyst potential).
- Armored stone plates (heavy warding or fortification craft).
- Dense fault-stone chunks (ritual anchors, pressure runes).

Ingredients:
- Molten-vein residue (high-risk alchemical reagent).
- Seismic dust (earth attunement blends, volatile in heat).
