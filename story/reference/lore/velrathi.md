# Velrathi

## Description
- Striking, near-supernatural beauty with idealized humanoid features
- Tall, slender, and graceful build
- Faint ember-like eye glow
- Subtle horn-like ridges along hairline
- Skin tones range from alabaster to deep obsidian with porcelain-like texture

## Core Traits
- Hypnotic social presence
- Natural affinity for pact and soul-binding magic
- Lifespans approximately ten times human baseline
- Aristocratic society centered on pacts, lineage, and prestige

## Cultural Alignment Pattern
- Many Velrathi lineages align with demonic patrons
- Dominant ideology frames desire/power as more truthful than human moral restraint
- Historic war doctrine included extermination and enslavement campaigns against human/elf aligned powers

## Current Canon Notes
- Not all Velrathi are ideologically uniform; documented defections exist
- High-rank defectors may possess critical lost knowledge of contract architecture and infernal claim mechanics
