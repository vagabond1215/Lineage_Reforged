# Magic and Supernatural Elements

## Lightning Magic
- Used by Arienne Valis
- High precision combat magic

## Elementals
- Earth spirits inhabiting mine structures
- Includes lesser and greater earth-form variants

## Contract Magic (Goddess of Justice)
- Granted to Sehir after divine intervention in the mine collapse
- Supports both mutual contracts (accepted by both parties) and unilateral punitive contracts (executed by offended party)
- Can enforce service, restitution, conduct limits, and structured compensation terms
- Exerts cognitive-emotional pressure away from violence/chaos and toward reparative alignment over time
- Generates residual restorative energy for the Goddess of Justice per valid enforcement

## Contract Constraint Notes
- Terms must resolve as just/equivalent under execution logic
- Parties may petition term adjustments, but unilateral execution authority remains with contract executor
