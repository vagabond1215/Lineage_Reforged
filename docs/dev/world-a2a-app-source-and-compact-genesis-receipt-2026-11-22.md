## Application source integration

The method lazily starts a dedicated Worker and terminates it after each result or failure. The Worker resolves the original algorithms through the verified browser loader. The ordinary launcher calls neither this method nor the Worker. A separate `world-source-review` build mode exposes the same method on exact loopback for browser QA, without adding a UI action. ## Field disposition and storage budget

The compact envelope is an executable **tick-zero candidate**. Its receipt remains `PREPARED_NOT_COMMITTED` and its geometry/content/scenario are fixed by the versioned source. The experiment retains the original world identity, account and lifecycle identity, intent, proposed World ID, authenticated creation metadata as supplied by the future owner, rules, zero clock/revision, empty association and mutation lists, full intent receipt metadata, scenario and v2 identity, projection digest, and the entire existing economy ledger with clock/source/settlement identity. The ledger is a conservative genesis input, including current estimates. Services and minted inventory stay false. It is not yet a versioned persisted form for evolved World state.

It explicitly rejects changed content and unmatched retained values. This checkpoint does not establish seed, binding to an authenticated account, versioned archive persistence, or recovery for evolved Worlds.
