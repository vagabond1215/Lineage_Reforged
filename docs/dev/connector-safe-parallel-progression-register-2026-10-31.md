## Key settled product direction that all tracks inherit

- **Default Current Area** for immediate gameplay, **existing Codex → Atlas** for larger known geography, no extra Home or duplicate World Atlas; map reads source, does not mint canonical places.
- **Typed location owners, not one universal mutable map database**; user approves developer-reviewed edits of existing canonical location profiles first, and welcomes source-backed improvement recommendations. Sites/districts remain settlement-relative until separately validated local geometry. World-generated places wait for world-instance identity, persistence and canonical collision/namespace rules. No generalized `world.pois`.
- **Discovered-only personal cartography**: player sees only personally discovered geography; an accessible possessed/posted chart may temporarily reveal *its own depiction* beyond that boundary, with only accepted vague bearings retained after loss of access. Map Study can refine already discovered land through future evidence, and crude charts can mislead. Do not ship undiscovered full-world raster/geometry in a normal player payload; viewer authorization must gate assets and text.
- **Native gear** owns physical item identity/Save as currently worked by Codex; no parallel editor, tool/equipment caller mutation, or hand/use owner.
- **World-first creation** with fixed committed seed before first character, **Live or Attended** clocks as future modes, finite queued actions and one world chronology; no current scheduler claimed.
- **Dungeon** ordinary mana capture/refinement/core facets/consciousness and leyline physical-source ownership separate from religion and observer knowledge; no actual node/dungeon placement from map shape alone.
- **No fake source**: ecological plausibility is not observable harvestable stock, a building/service descriptor is not an NPC/provider, a map region label is not exact position, and a save `location.*` compatibility key is not a new canonical settlement.
