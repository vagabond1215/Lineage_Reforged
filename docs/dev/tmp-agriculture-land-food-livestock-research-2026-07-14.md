## 1. Gate Result

The strongest conclusion is that agriculture must be represented as a set of bounded relationships among land, climate, water, canonical organisms, initial outputs, stabilization, storage, tools, workplaces, infrastructure, skills, and later production. A crop name is not a standing crop; a fauna record is not a domestic herd; an output key is not a producing animal state; a workplace output tag is not an executed harvest; and a regional supply description is not a field, stock, yield, or inventory instance.

The live repository already has unusually broad identity and descriptive coverage: 117 flora, 132 fauna, 9 regional ecology profiles, 41 regions, 47 region localities, 88 settlements, 1,372 items, 1,617 market values, 121 production chains, 58 workplaces, 22 extraction-method descriptors, 121 skills, 7 civilization-infrastructure records, and 28 Knowledge snippets. It also has abstract spatial supply and settlement-storage consumers. However, the flora/fauna scalar fields required for yields, population, reproduction, domestication, regrowth, and impact remain placeholders; only 12 planned recipes exist; and there is no general owner for fields, farm parcels, standing crops, herds, sowing, feeding, breeding, milking, shearing, irrigation execution, soil state, crop state, agricultural weather effects, farm-lot storage, spoilage, or agriculture-source/output item generation ([flora](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/flora.json), [fauna](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/fauna.json), [regional ecology](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/regional_ecology_profiles.json), [items](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/items/items.json), [market values](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/civilization/market_item_values.json), [production chains](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/civilization/production_chains.json), [workplaces](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/civilization/workplaces.json), [recipes](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/crafting/recipes.json), [spatial world](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/spatial-world.ts), [economy](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/economy.ts), [settlement simulation](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/settlement-simulation.ts), [world engine](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/world-engine/src/index.ts)).

External evidence supports the following integration posture:

1. Land capability is use-specific. Soil, slope, drainage, water, climate, access, labor, infrastructure, and management must be matched to a proposed land use; no universal `fertile` score is defensible ([FAO land evaluation](https://www.fao.org/4/X5310E/X5310E00.htm); [USDA land capability](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A-PURL-gpo20777/pdf/GOVPUB-A-PURL-gpo20777.pdf)).
2. Soil amendments are not interchangeable. Manure, compost, green manure, crop residues, ash, lime, and marl have different sources, preparation, transport, hazards, and site dependencies ([FAO composting](https://www.fao.org/4/y5104e/y5104e00.htm); [FAO plant nutrition](https://www.fao.org/4/a0443e/a0443e.pdf); [Pitman](https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/cpl041)).
3. Irrigation, drainage, water lifting, and terrace maintenance are distinct infrastructure relationships. Added water can intensify waterlogging or salinity when drainage, outlet, maintenance, and local hydrology are incompatible ([FAO drainage](https://www.fao.org/4/a0975e/a0975e.pdf); [FAO salt-affected soils](https://www.fao.org/4/x5871e/x5871e00.htm); [Fraenkel](https://www.fao.org/4/ah810e/AH810E00.htm)).
4. Crop systems should be built only from repository-canonical flora and outputs. External crops and historical systems are analogues, never new canon.
5. Rotation, mixed cropping, fodder, residue grazing, manure return, and crop-livestock integration are conditional relationships, not automatic bonuses or one universal three-field package ([Hamerow et al.](https://academic.oup.com/book/61548/chapter/537298429); [Schiere and Kater](https://www.fao.org/4/Y0501E/y0501e00.htm); [Rakkar and Blanco-Canqui](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2017.11.018)).
6. Milk, eggs, wool, honey, manure, and traction require an eligible canonical source plus appropriate state, feed, water, health, equipment, handling, and storage. Names, aliases, and output arrays establish candidate relationships, not executable production ([FAO/IDF dairy guide](https://www.fao.org/4/ba0027e/ba0027e00.pdf); [FAO poultry](https://www.fao.org/4/Y5169e/y5169e00.htm); [Petrie](https://www.fao.org/4/v9384e/v9384e10.htm); [FAO good beekeeping](https://doi.org/10.4060/cb5353en); [Brandjes et al.](https://www.fao.org/4/x6113e/x6113e00.htm); [WOAH working equids](https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahc/2024/en_chapitre_aw_working_equids.htm)).
7. Harvest, threshing, winnowing, cleaning, drying, seed selection, haymaking, milking hygiene, egg collection, shearing, and initial sorting are farm-adjacent stabilization. Milling, baking, brewing, dairy manufacture, rendering, tanning, dyeing, oil making, and detailed food processing remain later-gate work ([FAO wheat post-harvest](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/inpho/docs/Post_Harvest_Compendium_-_WHEAT.pdf); [FAO small-scale postharvest](https://www.fao.org/4/ae075e/ae075e05.htm); [Suttie](https://www.fao.org/4/X7660E/X7660E00.htm); [FAO/IDF dairy guide](https://www.fao.org/4/ba0027e/ba0027e00.pdf); [FAO poultry](https://www.fao.org/4/Y5169e/y5169e00.htm); [Petrie](https://www.fao.org/4/v9384e/v9384e10.htm); [Brandjes et al.](https://www.fao.org/4/x6113e/x6113e00.htm)).
8. Storage is commodity- and climate-specific. This gate can support requirements and hazards, but not capacity, pest rolls, spoilage timers, loss rates, or inventory behavior ([GTZ grain-loss manual](https://www.fao.org/4/x5065e/x5065E00.htm); [Proctor](https://www.fao.org/4/t1838e/t1838e00.htm)).
9. Regional differences must alter actual dependencies: available land, rainfall, drainage, pasture, winter or dry-season fodder, storage, transport, and imports. They cannot be cosmetic renames of one generic agricultural package.
10. Ordinary technology remains primary. Magic may only supply bounded indication, warning, limited lift, temporary holding, drying, lighting, calming, observation, or warding through an accepted canon route; it cannot provide free crops, instant growth, guaranteed rain, unlimited fertility, or a bypass around land, water, fodder, labor, maintenance, scarcity, ecology, or ownership.

This gate authorizes no records or behavior. It supplies evidence, corrections, and candidate dispositions for the later unversioned cross-domain integration.

### Method

- **Repository verification:** private files were opened through the connector at the observed commit; local parsing reproduced counts, ids, cross-references, placeholder state, and runtime consumers. Repository links in this report point to the connected private repository.
- **External research:** searches prioritized intergovernmental and government technical material, peer-reviewed research, formal scholarly synthesis, and bounded historical or archaeological evidence. Search snippets were never used as evidence without a direct source.
- **Collision posture:** existing ids, output keys, item/value coverage, workplaces, chains, skills, infrastructure, regions, and runtime owners were checked before any candidate was classified.
- **No implementation inference:** a relationship supported by evidence remains an integration candidate until the later cross-domain pass accepts its owner, schema posture, canonical sources, downstream consumers, and regional authorship.

### Claim authority

- **Repository fact:** directly verified in connected repository material; controlling for current project state.
- **External evidence:** a cited real-world relationship or bounded case; informative but non-canonical.
- **Design inference:** an explicit repo-compatible interpretation, not a fact or implementation instruction.
- **Integration candidate:** a possible identity, relationship, correction, precondition, authored question, or reservation for later disposition.
- **Runtime reservation:** mutable behavior or generated state that no static document may silently authorize.

### External-source quality

- **A1 - primary authority:** binding or first-party technical standard or code from the responsible authority.
- **A2 - peer reviewed:** journal research, scholarly synthesis, or reviewed archaeological/historical analysis.
- **B1 - official evidence:** government or intergovernmental manual, framework, technical guide, or official evidence synthesis.
- **B2 - institutional evidence:** university, professional, extension, or formal development material with narrower authority.
- **C - contextual analogue:** a well-documented but site-specific analogue useful only for constraints or possibilities.

Modern agricultural, welfare, food-safety, and engineering sources support physical dependencies and hazards, not modern institutions, exact thresholds, production values, or universal availability. Historical and archaeological sources demonstrate compatible possibilities, not repository culture, law, or automatic canon. Transferability limits appear in Section 20.

### Existing runtime consumers and the precise gap

The civilization engine derives 305 flora/fauna/mineral source descriptors (117 + 132 + 56), hex availability, settlement resource access, and supply capability across 47 world hexes and 88 settlements. Arable and pasture catchments can influence supply capability. The economy engine derives per-tick macro supply/demand for authored settlement goods, including generic seasonal factors for grain, fruit, vegetables, and herbs; this is economy abstraction, not species crop growth or yield. Runtime economy performs live chain/workplace-based craft and value resolution, including the mixed-crop chain, but does not consume or mutate field, animal, inventory, or lot state. Settlement simulation derives abstract agrarian labor weight, transport-animal capability, farm-estate and pasture-run business context, rural/storage districts, and granary/cellar/warehouse/vault capacity, load, and utilization. Transport and trade runtimes validate configured animal/harness/vehicle combinations and execute abstract route/cargo transport. These are real spatial-supply, craft/value, economic, transport, and abstract settlement-storage owners; the report must not claim that the repository has no agriculture-related economic resolution, transport, or storage runtime ([spatial world](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/spatial-world.ts), [resource taxonomy](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/resource-taxonomy.ts), [economy](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/economy.ts), [runtime economy](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/runtime-economy.ts), [settlement simulation](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/settlement-simulation.ts), [transport runtime](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/transport-runtime.ts), [trade runtime](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/trade-runtime.ts)).

Those consumers do not create fields or herds, load extraction descriptors as actions, mutate soil or water, plant or harvest crops, feed or breed animals, collect domestic outputs, create item instances, manage seed/fodder/crop lots, roll pests or spoilage, or persist farm state. The shared session contract contains `weatherState`, while the world engine only emits a minimal `climateProfileId`/tick weather delta and does not own agricultural weather effects ([shared contracts](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/shared/types/src/contracts.ts), [world engine](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/world-engine/src/index.ts)). The exact gap is therefore: **the repository has static agriculture vocabulary plus derived spatial supply, settlement economy, transport, and abstract storage, but no crop/soil/water/livestock/farm-lot execution owner or animal-level agricultural work state.**

Gate 3 adds four qualifications:

1. The qualitative flora agronomy arrays can identify possible land, season, companion, soil, water, and tool relationships, but cannot act as crop calendars, cultivability, yields, or field simulation.
2. Fauna names, aliases, diet categories, and output arrays can identify verification candidates, but cannot establish domestication, producing state, draft readiness, husbandry, or output collection.

Gate 2's residue filter still applies; repeated use, trade value, hazard, ritual significance, or narrative value is required before a separate item identity is justified ([Muscat et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gfs.2019.100330); [Schiere and Kater](https://www.fao.org/4/Y0501E/y0501e00.htm)).
5. The current runtime economy consumes production-chain/workplace profiles for live market and craft-value resolution. Earlier “macro/descriptive” language remains valid only as a warning against inheriting exact recipe batch authority or pretending that economic resolution is physical field/inventory execution; it is not a statement that chains have no runtime consumer ([runtime economy](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/runtime-economy.ts), [recipe reconciliation](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/docs/design/0.6.5-research-prerequisite-and-recipe-authority-reconciliation.md)).

Gate 3 does not resolve Gate 2's ox-milk, shared bird-output, antler, shell/scute, roe, oil-stage, monster-parallel, or pearl/nacre issues. It carries them forward for verification and refuses to use them as implementation authority.

## 6. Land Capability, Soil, Water, And Land-Use Evidence

### Land capability is a relationship, not an intrinsic score

FAO land evaluation matches the qualities of a land unit to the requirements of a specified land use. Climate, soil, terrain, water, management, infrastructure, and socio-economic context all matter; a tract cannot be ranked from universally best to worst without naming the proposed use and management system. USDA land-capability guidance likewise separates erosion, wetness, root-zone, slope, salinity, and climatic limits ([FAO Framework](https://www.fao.org/4/X5310E/X5310E00.htm), B1; [Klingebiel and Montgomery](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A-PURL-gpo20777/pdf/GOVPUB-A-PURL-gpo20777.pdf), B1).

For this repository, the safe static relationship is:

`canonical place + land-use proposal + climate/soil/slope/drainage/water/access constraints + available tools/labor/infrastructure -> qualitative compatibility or authored uncertainty`

It is not:

`region or biome tag -> farm, field, crop, yield, owner, or action`

The locality catalog already distinguishes `arableLand` and `pasture` bands. At the baseline, arable bands are none 16, scarce 7, limited 8, moderate 6, strong 7, and surplus 3; pasture bands are none 9, scarce 7, limited 18, moderate 5, strong 6, and surplus 2. The spatial engine consumes those bands as derived settlement supply capability. They are the best live qualitative land-access signals, but they do not place parcels or authorize a universal land score ([localities](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/region_localities.json), [spatial world](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/spatial-world.ts)).

Archaeology further cautions against reducing farming to one arable template. A Maya Lowlands synthesis documents terraces, wetland modifications, managed succession, gardens, and tree crops as different forms of long-term landscape investment whose viability depended on maintenance and local conditions. This proves diversity of land-use strategies, not a transferable Maya package or repository culture ([Fedick, Morell-Hart, and Dussol](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10814-023-09185-z), A2). Medieval Iberian archaeobotany similarly supports gardens and orchards as managed settings distinct from broad arable fields, but its species and practices cannot be imported into canon ([Pena-Chocarro and Perez-Jorda](https://doi.org/10.1111/emed.12348), A2).

### Soil, fertility, and amendment distinctions

Soil texture, structure, depth, organic matter, drainage, slope position, salinity/acidity, climate, vegetation, and disturbance interact. Organic matter can affect aggregation, porosity, infiltration, water retention, biological activity, and nutrient availability, but the magnitude and direction vary by soil and management. That supports qualitative soil-condition and amendment relationships, not a fertility meter, universal bonus, depletion formula, or recovery timer ([Bot and Benites](https://www.fao.org/4/a0100e/a0100e00.htm), B1; [USDA NRCS soil health](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/soil/soil-health), B1).

The following distinctions should be preserved:

| Input or practice | Evidence-supported relationship | Gate boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fallow / cover / retained residues | Can alter cover, erosion exposure, organic inputs, weeds, water, and later cropping; effects depend on system and climate | Static relationship or authored practice only; no automatic recovery ([USDA NRCS soil health](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/soil/soil-health), B1) |
| Legume rotation / green manure | May contribute biological nitrogen and other rotation effects, but outcome depends on crop sequence, soil, water, residue handling, and management | Never “legume restores fertility” as a universal rule ([Lotjonen and Ollikainen](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41130-018-0063-z), A2) |
| Raw manure | Dung/urine plus collection and storage context; can return nutrients and organic matter but also creates handling and pollution hazards | Separate from bedding waste and compost; no nutrient values ([Maillard and Angers](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.12438), A2; [Brandjes et al.](https://www.fao.org/4/x6113e/x6113e00.htm), B1) |
| Bedding waste | Bedding mixed with dung/urine and contamination; feedstock or waste depending on handling | Not automatically stable amendment ([Brandjes et al.](https://www.fao.org/4/x6113e/x6113e00.htm), B1; [FAO composting](https://www.fao.org/4/y5104e/y5104e00.htm), B1) |
| Compost | Managed transformation dependent on feedstock mix, moisture, aeration, temperature, siting, time, and labor | Later material/process relationship; no timer or guaranteed safety ([Misra, Roy, and Hiraoka](https://www.fao.org/4/y5104e/y5104e00.htm), B1) |
| Ash | Source- and combustion-dependent alkaline/mineral residue with possible liming value and salt/trace-element hazards | Gate 1 source and residue audit required; no generic fertilizer item ([Pitman](https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/cpl041), A2) |
| Lime / marl | Geological material whose suitability depends on source, reactivity, fineness, soil condition, transport, and application knowledge | Requires canonical geological source and later material authority ([FAO plant nutrition](https://www.fao.org/4/a0443e/a0443e.pdf), B1) |
| Terrace / contour / cover | Can reduce runoff and erosion under compatible slope, construction, drainage, cover, and maintenance | Infrastructure relationship, never an unconditional terrain bonus ([Chen, Wei, and Chen](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2017.08.007), A2; [USDA soil conservation](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/soil-conservation-cover-crops-mulches-tillage-and-cultivation), B1) |

The flora schema's `rotationBenefit`, companion, antagonist, mulch, raised-bed, irrigation, greenhouse, and trellis vocabulary shows that the data model anticipates some of these ideas, but the placeholder scalar state and highly repeated five-species companion topology make it unsafe to treat the template as validated agronomy. The integration should audit those relationships before promotion, not generate bonuses from them ([flora](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/flora.json), [flora schema](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/schemas/world/flora.schema.json)).

### Water, irrigation, and drainage

Rain-fed farming, seasonal flood use, wells, springs, diversions, ditches, terraces, raised beds, cisterns, ponds, canals, and water lifting solve different problems. Their compatibility depends on source reliability and quality, topography, lift head, soils, drainage outlet, sediment, salinity, evaporation, frost, flood exposure, construction materials, power, maintenance, labor, and institutional coordination. Water lifting may be manual, animal-powered, water-powered, or otherwise mechanized, but the existence of a device in technical literature establishes only a possibility; it does not establish local availability or throughput ([FAO Framework](https://www.fao.org/4/X5310E/X5310E00.htm), B1; [FAO drainage](https://www.fao.org/4/a0975e/a0975e.pdf), B1; [Price](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12520-024-02082-6), A2; [Fraenkel](https://www.fao.org/4/ah810e/AH810E00.htm), B1).

Irrigation and drainage must not be collapsed. Waterlogging can follow excess irrigation, seepage, inadequate drainage, or an incompatible water table. Salinity and sodicity are distinct soil-water problems; leaching salts requires an adequate outlet and safe drainage, while adding water without drainage may worsen both waterlogging and salinization ([FAO environmental considerations](https://www.fao.org/4/w4347e/w4347e10.htm), B1; [Abrol, Yadav, and Massoud](https://www.fao.org/4/x5871e/x5871e00.htm), B1). Drainage planning consequently requires hydrology, topography, soil, climate, irrigation practice, outlet, construction, monitoring, maintenance, environment, and participating institutions; it is not a generic ditch tag ([van der Molen, Martinez Beltran, and Ochs](https://www.fao.org/4/a0975e/a0975e.pdf), B1).

Canals and other shared works create two maintenance scales: periodic institutional work and routine user upkeep. A modern Egyptian case demonstrates that distinction but cannot establish Lineage ownership, taxation, law, or labor organization ([Barnes](https://doi.org/10.1177/0263775816655161), A2). Raised fields demonstrate one possible response to high water tables and floodplain conditions, yet salinity, drainage, oversaturation, heat, layout, and maintenance remain controlling; the Chimú-period Casma Valley evidence is a bounded analogue, not a universal wetland improvement ([Price](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12520-024-02082-6), A2).

The live `infrastructure.irrigation_works` is therefore an appropriate static home for qualitative service and construction dependencies, but not for field moisture, water allocation, pumping, channel flow, crop response, degradation, or repair execution. Those would require explicit future runtime owners ([infrastructure](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/civilization/infrastructure.json)).

## 7. Crop And Staple-System Evidence

### Canonical identity controls the analysis

Only live flora and item identities may become repository candidates. A familiar crop name does not by itself prove cultivation, local availability, a seed route, or a usable output; stronger support comes from a combination of canonical flora, resolved outputs, regional ecology, settlement roles, workplace/chain vocabulary, and later authored decisions. External species and historical crop packages remain analogues.

### Crop establishment, harvest, and residues

FAO seed-system guidance distinguishes selection, multiplication, access, storage, and quality in local and formal seed pathways. Seed and consumption grain may be biologically identical while differing in intended use, purity, viability, vigor, contamination, drying, and handling. This supports a seed-stock relationship, not mandatory certification, landrace ids, germination values, or seed inventory simulation ([FAO seed systems](https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/thematic-sitemap/theme/compendium/tools-guidelines/what-are-seed-systems/en/), B1; [van Gastel, Bishaw, and Gregg](https://www.fao.org/4/y4011e/y4011e0v.htm), B1).

The general crop route should remain conceptual:

`canonical plant + compatible land/water/season + selected propagule + tools/skill -> standing crop [no owner] -> crop-specific harvest part -> field sorting/cleaning/drying or curing -> separated seed/food/fodder/material lots [no owner] -> later processing or trade`

Harvest residues are not one class. Cereal straw, husks, chaff, stalks, orchard prunings, pomace, pulse haulms, spoiled produce, wash water, and rejected material have different source stages and possible destinations. The Gate 2 residue filter controls identity: repeated downstream use, trade value, hazard, ritual significance, or strong narrative value must exist before a separate record is justified ([Baloch](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/inpho/docs/Post_Harvest_Compendium_-_WHEAT.pdf), B1; [Muscat et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gfs.2019.100330), A2; [Gate 2](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/docs/dev/tmp-ecology-flora-fauna-byproducts-research-2026-07-14.md)).

Post-harvest evidence also prevents a universal crop treatment. Cereal harvest, threshing, winnowing/cleaning, drying, and storage are distinct stages ([Baloch](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/inpho/docs/Post_Harvest_Compendium_-_WHEAT.pdf), B1). Roots, tubers, and bulbs remain living produce after harvest; digging injury, handling, curing, ventilation, temperature, and moisture requirements differ by commodity ([FAO root/tuber storage](https://www.fao.org/4/X5415E/x5415e03.htm), B1). Fruits and vegetables vary in maturity pattern, mechanical sensitivity, water loss, and cooling or ventilation need; these relationships support crop-specific stabilization questions but no exact times, temperatures, or loss rates ([FAO small-scale postharvest](https://www.fao.org/4/ae075e/ae075e04.htm), B1).

## 8. Rotation, Mixed Farming, Fodder, And Pasture Evidence

### Rotation and mixed systems

Historical evidence from England c. 700-1300 supports seasonal sowing, fallow, grazing, mixed crops, and changing rotation practices, while also showing chronological and regional variation. It therefore rejects a universal European three-field template rather than authorizing one ([Hamerow et al.](https://academic.oup.com/book/61548/chapter/537298429), A2). Modern global meta-analysis similarly finds crop-rotation effects vary with climate, soil texture, and agronomy; it can support conditional diversity/sequence relationships but not medieval values or guaranteed benefits ([Liu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2022.108017), A2).

A mixed crop-livestock farm can connect fields, crop residues, pasture, animals, manure, draft power, household needs, and markets. The same integration also creates competition: land, labor, water, grain, residues, and transport have rival uses. Outcomes depend on rainfall, soil, land area, capital, labor, animal condition, feed base, institutions, and scale ([Schiere and Kater](https://www.fao.org/4/Y0501E/y0501e00.htm), B1). The static model should therefore record possible links and prerequisites, never an automatic circular bonus.

Conditional relationship candidates include:

- cereal followed by or mixed with a canonical pulse/legume, where climate, crop sequence, water, and authored practice support it;
- fallow or temporary ley grazed under compatible stocking and soil conditions;
- crop residues routed to feed, bedding, soil cover, compost feedstock, fuel, construction, paper, or waste;
- collected manure/bedding routed to storage and later amendment preparation;
- orchard or field-edge vegetation supplying ground cover, browse, habitat, or harvested products where authored;
- gardens, orchards, pasture, meadow, vineyard, wetland plots, and broad arable fields as distinct land-use relationships;
- household, village, estate, guild/temple, and strategic systems differing in labor concentration, transport, storage, maintenance, and coordination.

Residue grazing is not a universal benefit. Soil moisture, compaction risk, stocking, residue removal, erosion cover, and later crop needs condition the result ([Rakkar and Blanco-Canqui](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2017.11.018), A2). Biomass competition is real: feed, bedding, soil cover, fuel, construction, and industry may demand the same residue ([Muscat et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gfs.2019.100330), A2).

### Fodder and pasture are not one item

Pasture, browse, hay, straw, crop residues, fodder crops, grain/pulse feed, mast, and mineral/water access are distinct. Their seasonal availability, storage behavior, palatability, feed quality, and suitability vary by species and environment. Conserved hay and suitable residues can bridge winter, drought, or lean periods, but require timely cutting/drying, protection from rewetting, and space; straw is not nutritionally equivalent to hay or fresh pasture ([Suttie](https://www.fao.org/4/X7660E/X7660E00.htm), B1).

Leaf fodder in northwest Greek mountains and medieval swine pannage in southeast England demonstrate two bounded historical possibilities: stored tree leaf/twig fodder and seasonal woodland mast with droving/water/access dependencies. Neither example authorizes those plants, practices, institutions, or laws in repository cultures ([Halstead](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0956793300001588), A2; [Margetts](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10163354/2/Margetts_In%20the%20time%20of%20mast-%20Investigating%20the%20medieval%20swine%20pannage%20of%20south-east%20England_VoR.pdf), A2).

The live locality pasture bands, regional ecology, fauna diets, Farmstead, Sheepfold, and pastoral extraction descriptor provide a starting vocabulary. They do not resolve which canonical plant feeds which canonical animal, whether an animal is domesticated, what is eaten, how much, or when ([localities](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/region_localities.json), [regional ecology](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/regional_ecology_profiles.json), [fauna](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/fauna.json), [workplaces](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/civilization/workplaces.json)).

## 9. Livestock, Domestic Outputs, Traction, And Husbandry Evidence

### Canonical animals and the domestication gate

Regional ecology explicitly supports cattle, horse, sheep, and goat in several macro supply contexts, while settlements explicitly author macro roles including horse breeding, animal husbandry, stock raising, and cattle raising. Other plausible domestic or semi-managed names are present, including ox, ram, marsh goat, donkey, mule, swine, chicken, domestic duck, goose, honey bee, barn cat, herding hound, and rabbit. Regional ecology and explicit settlement roles are therefore strong macro authored support, but neither establishes fauna-level `domesticatable` or `mountable` values, a producing state, or executable animal actions. Those scalars remain placeholders, and other domestication or management claims remain verification or authored-input questions ([regional ecology](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/regional_ecology_profiles.json), [settlements](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/settlements.json), [fauna](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/fauna.json)).

The safe prerequisite shape is:

`verified canonical animal + accepted managed/domestic relationship + eligible life/sex/health/season state + feed/water/shelter/handling + appropriate tool/workplace + collection or work action [no owner] -> raw output or service [no generated instance]`

### Domestic outputs

- **Milk:** a milk output key does not prove lactation, continuous production, quantity, or collection. Dairy guidance supports healthy eligible animals, adequate feed and water, hygienic milking, clean equipment, prompt cooling or delivery, and contamination control. It is modern safety guidance and supplies no canonical species, yields, or processing recipe ([FAO and IDF](https://www.fao.org/4/ba0027e/ba0027e00.pdf), B1).
- **Eggs and feathers:** poultry production depends on suitable birds/flock state, feed, water, housing/ventilation, health, predator protection, litter management, collection, sorting, and storage. Village-poultry guidance is not a universal housing or output model ([Sonaiya and Swan](https://www.fao.org/4/Y5169e/y5169e00.htm), B1).
- **Wool/fiber:** a canonical fiber-output relationship still requires an eligible fiber-bearing animal, growth/season condition, shearing or collection, contamination control, sorting/skirting, and packing. Commercial fiber guidance supplies handling relationships, not species identity or production rates ([Petrie](https://www.fao.org/4/v9384e/v9384e10.htm), B1).
- **Hair:** no live fauna output or item identity establishes an agricultural hair lane. Hair must not be inferred from wool, fur, hide, an animal name, or external husbandry. Any later candidate requires a canonical source, repeated consumer, item/value/chain collision audit, and Gate 4 material disposition; otherwise it remains deferred ([fauna](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/fauna.json), [items](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/items/items.json)).
- **Honey and wax:** managed beekeeping requires a verified social-bee source, forage and water, suitable hive/site/equipment, colony health, monitoring, and careful harvest. It cannot be inferred solely from a bee name, and the current apiculture descriptor's `flora` domain merits correction review ([FAO et al.](https://doi.org/10.4060/cb5353en), B1; [methods](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/civilization/extraction_methods.json)).
- **Manure and bedding waste:** dung, urine, bedding, collection, exposure, storage, runoff, and composting state change both value and hazard. Raw manure is neither clean inventory nor stable compost ([Brandjes et al.](https://www.fao.org/4/x6113e/x6113e00.htm), B1).
- **Slaughter outputs:** live fauna relationships include source-specific meat, hide/skin, bone, horn, feathers, and other verified outputs, but blood, fat, manure, guano, sinew, tendon, and offal are absent from fauna output relationships. Those absent streams remain conditional Gate 2 consumer/collision questions, not existing source-specific outputs. Slaughter action, carcass state, butchery, stabilization, rendering, tanning, and inventory generation are not authorized here ([fauna](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/fauna.json), [Gate 2](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/docs/dev/tmp-ecology-flora-fauna-byproducts-research-2026-07-14.md)).

The live `workplace.sheepfold` emits generic `wool_fleece` and `milk_raw` without a fauna-source relationship; `apiary_yard` emits `honeycomb` and `beeswax`; Farmstead emits crop/grain/straw and orchard outputs. These should be audited as relationship gaps, not treated as executed production ([workplaces](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/civilization/workplaces.json)).

### Traction, transport, and husbandry

Draft or pack capability requires more than an alias. A candidate animal must be eligible, healthy, trained, conditioned, fed, watered, sheltered, rested, and handled by skilled people. A fitted and maintained yoke or harness, compatible implement or vehicle, suitable terrain/road/field, and repair capacity are separate prerequisites. WOAH's working-equid code directly supports fitness, feed, water, shelter, rest, harness fit, climate adjustment, and handling for equids; its detailed provisions must not be transferred to oxen or other species ([WOAH](https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahc/2024/en_chapitre_aw_working_equids.htm), A1).

FAO transport/traction guidance supports the broader animal-implement-vehicle-infrastructure-operator-maintenance relationship, while Ethiopian highland evidence demonstrates how seasonal feed constraints and work scheduling can interact for draught oxen. Neither supplies universal work rates, prices, diets, or institutions ([Crossley, Chamen, and Kienzle](https://www.fao.org/4/i0525e/i0525e.pdf), B1; [Goe and Reed](https://www.fao.org/4/x5493e/x5493e18.htm), B1). Harness design, fit, and maintenance can affect injury and performance, but a Sudanese cart-equid context cannot establish one universal harness or yoke ([Hovell](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-1591%2898%2900116-6), A2).

Husbandry also creates qualitative failure pressures: feed or water shortage, heat, cold, drought, mud, disease/parasites, young-animal care, predators, theft, conflict, shelter failure, and labor shortage. A climate-impact review supports direct heat/water stress and indirect forage/disease effects with substantial species and regional variation ([Escarcha, Lassa, and Zander](https://doi.org/10.3390/cli6030054), A2). A Greek wolf case supports surveillance, night confinement, and guardian practices as context-dependent depredation mitigations, never guaranteed protection or canonical husbandry law ([Petridou et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2023.110097), A2).

This gate rejects work-rate formulas, stamina, labor queues, speeds, plowing execution, feed quantities, breeding timers, output rates, disease mechanics, and animal wear.

## 10. Initial Farm Processing And Storage

### Farm-adjacent stabilization boundary

Accepted as evidence-supported relationship vocabulary, subject to canonical source and identity checks. The stage families below are supported respectively by cereal and fresh-produce handling, hay conservation, dairy and poultry handling, fiber harvesting, manure management, and the Gate 2 biological boundary; none supplies an action, quantity, or item-creation rule ([Baloch](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/inpho/docs/Post_Harvest_Compendium_-_WHEAT.pdf), B1; [FAO post-harvest manual](https://www.fao.org/4/t0073e/t0073e01.htm), B1; [Suttie](https://www.fao.org/4/X7660E/X7660E00.htm), B1; [FAO/IDF](https://www.fao.org/4/ba0027e/ba0027e00.pdf), B1; [FAO poultry](https://www.fao.org/4/Y5169e/y5169e00.htm), B1; [Petrie](https://www.fao.org/4/v9384e/v9384e10.htm), B1; [Brandjes et al.](https://www.fao.org/4/x6113e/x6113e00.htm), B1; [Gate 2](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/docs/dev/tmp-ecology-flora-fauna-byproducts-research-2026-07-14.md)):

- cutting, lifting, picking, binding, field sorting, and gentle handling;
- cereal threshing, shelling, cleaning, winnowing, drying, grading, and seed selection;
- root/tuber cleaning, damage sorting, crop-appropriate curing, and draining;
- fruit/vegetable sorting, trimming, shade or prompt cooling where compatible;
- hay cutting, turning/drying, baling/stacking, and protection from rewetting;
- milk collection hygiene and prompt cooling/delivery;
- egg collection, cleaning/sorting as appropriate, and separate storage;
- fleece shearing/collection, skirting, sorting, drying if needed, and packing;
- manure/bedding collection and separation from food/feed/water;
- hide stabilization only as a boundary question; detailed tanning remains Gate 4;
- pressing or crushing only as identification of a next-stage interface, not a recipe or process authorization.

The evidence for cereals separates harvest, threshing, winnowing/cleaning, drying, grading, and protected storage ([Baloch](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/inpho/docs/Post_Harvest_Compendium_-_WHEAT.pdf), B1; [Fellows, Axtell, and Dillon](https://www.fao.org/4/v5380e/v5380e00.htm), B1). Fresh roots, tubers, fruits, and vegetables demand commodity-specific handling because mechanical injury, water loss, continued respiration, and decay pressures differ ([FAO post-harvest manual](https://www.fao.org/4/t0073e/t0073e01.htm), B1). These relationships do not authorize detailed milling, baking, brewing, dairy manufacture, rendering, tanning, dyeing, oil production, slaughter processing, or crafting.

### Storage forms and pressures

Storage form must follow the material and climate. Grain stores need clean, dry, protected structures and attention to moisture, heat, insects, rodents, birds, water entry, and lot separation. Raised stores, bins, jars, sacks, baskets, sealed pits, and ventilated buildings address different constraints; pits are plausible only where soil, groundwater, rainfall, drainage, sealing, and access permit ([Gwinner, Harnisch, and Muck](https://www.fao.org/4/x5065e/x5065E00.htm), B2; [Proctor](https://www.fao.org/4/t1838e/t1838e00.htm), B1). Archaeological insect evidence from one Gran Canaria granary confirms that long-term storage pest pressure is historically real, but it cannot supply universal pest taxa or repository storage culture ([Henriquez-Valido et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jas.2020.105179), A2).

Seed stock needs separation from consumption and trade lots to protect intended use, identity, and viability ([FAO seed systems](https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/thematic-sitemap/theme/compendium/tools-guidelines/what-are-seed-systems/en/), B1; [van Gastel, Bishaw, and Gregg](https://www.fao.org/4/y4011e/y4011e0v.htm), B1). Hay and straw need dry storage and protection from rewetting; moist hay can self-heat and create a fire hazard, but the evidence does not authorize ignition thresholds, probabilities, or timers ([Suttie](https://www.fao.org/4/X7660E/X7660E00.htm), B1; [Rothbaum](https://doi.org/10.1002/jctb.5010130704), A2). Milk and eggs require distinct hygiene and prompt handling ([FAO/IDF dairy guide](https://www.fao.org/4/ba0027e/ba0027e00.pdf), B1; [FAO poultry](https://www.fao.org/4/Y5169e/y5169e00.htm), B1); manure and wastes require physical separation from food, feed, and water ([Brandjes et al.](https://www.fao.org/4/x6113e/x6113e00.htm), B1). Passive evaporative cooling can provide bounded short-term fruit/vegetable cooling only under compatible temperature, humidity, water, design, and commodity conditions; it is not refrigeration and does not apply universally to grain, meat, or dairy ([Defraeye et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/frfst.2023.1100181), A2).

The repository's settlement simulation already owns abstract granary/cellar/warehouse/vault capacity, load, and utilization. A future farm-storage design must either reference that abstraction or define a deliberately separate lot-level owner; it must not duplicate capacity or silently convert the semantic Granary Courts district, workplace seed stores, or Knowledge snippets into mutable inventory ([settlement simulation](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/engines/civilization-engine/src/settlement-simulation.ts), [settlement districts](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/world/settlement_districts.json), [Knowledge snippets](https://github.com/vagabond1215/Lineage_Reforged/blob/master/packages/content/base/player/knowledge_snippets.json)).

## 12. Technology Compatibility Without A Century Label

### Compatibility tests

Any agricultural capability should be evaluated through the following chain rather than a century label:

1. **Biophysical fit:** canonical crop/animal biology, climate, season, soil, slope, water, drainage, salinity, frost, heat, humidity, wind, and disease/pest pressure.
2. **Material fit:** wood, stone, metal, fiber, containers, seed/propagules, feed, bedding, fuel, and repair materials.
3. **Power and transport:** human labor, verified draft animals, water power, road/field condition, lift head, flow, hauling distance, and vehicle/implement fit.
4. **Knowledge and labor:** identification, propagation, animal handling, construction, maintenance, hygiene, storage, scheduling, and specialist concentration.
5. **Settlement and institutional fit:** household access, shared works, storage, markets, security, labor coordination, and maintenance responsibility.
6. **Operating fit:** intended scale, seasonal bottlenecks, ordinary failures, replacement parts, waste handling, and downstream consumers.

These tests synthesize use-specific land evaluation, power and water-lift constraints, animal/equipment/route compatibility, storage conditions, mixed-system tradeoffs, and shared-maintenance evidence. They are a compatibility screen, not a universal technology progression ([FAO land evaluation](https://www.fao.org/4/X5310E/X5310E00.htm), B1; [Fraenkel](https://www.fao.org/4/ah810e/AH810E00.htm), B1; [WOAH working equids](https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahc/2024/en_chapitre_aw_working_equids.htm), A1; [Crossley, Chamen, and Kienzle](https://www.fao.org/4/i0525e/i0525e.pdf), B1; [Proctor](https://www.fao.org/4/t1838e/t1838e00.htm), B1; [Schiere and Kater](https://www.fao.org/4/Y0501E/y0501e00.htm), B1; [Barnes](https://doi.org/10.1177/0263775816655161), A2).

### Capability bands

| Band | Defensible capability posture | Examples of questions, not grants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Household / subsistence | Hand tools, small garden, a few verified animals, baskets/jars, local seed, limited storage | Is water close? Is seed retained? Is there winter/dry-season feed? Who maintains shelter? |
| 2. Village / ordinary town | Shared threshing, wells/ditches, small mill access, communal or household stores, local draft team | Are drainage and outlet maintained? Are lots separated? Are repair skills and fodder available? |
| 3. Urban specialist | Market gardens, dairying/poultry/APIARY specialists, millers, vintners, storage/handling services | Does urban demand compete for land/water/feed? How are perishables moved and wastes separated? |
| 4. Estate / guild / temple / academy / military / major merchant | Concentrated labor, orchards/vineyards, managed pasture, larger waterworks, specialist storage and transport | What canonical institution owns access and maintenance? What prevents universal availability? |
| 5. Strategic / capital-scale | Major canals, reservoirs/cistern networks, large granary systems, coordinated transport and security | Does topography permit it? Who repairs it? What happens when coordination or supply fails? |
| 6. Rare / exceptional / relic / legendary | Unusual mundane works or bounded magical assistance with scarce expertise and infrastructure | What finite vessel, recharge, area/volume, failure, security, and institutional gate applies? |

Historical existence proves possibility only after the compatibility chain is satisfied. Water-lifting devices, terraces, raised fields, sealed pits, pannage, leaf fodder, and large stores all remain conditional examples ([Fraenkel](https://www.fao.org/4/ah810e/AH810E00.htm); [Chen, Wei, and Chen](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2017.08.007); [Price](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12520-024-02082-6); [Halstead](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0956793300001588)).

### Owner boundary

Static content may own stable identity, broad compatibility, source-to-output topology, prerequisite relationships, place descriptions, infrastructure/workplace/tool/skill vocabulary, and bounded lore. Authored input must own regional land uses, crop/animal management traditions, institutions, rights, sacred/taboo practices, and intended exceptions. A future agriculture-specific runtime, or deliberately integrated existing owner, must own agriculture commands, eligibility evaluation, time, labor, resource consumption, weather and soil/water effects, crop stage, herd/flock state, feed, breeding, health, output generation, quality, hazards, stock lots, spoilage, loss, agriculture-driven market response, inventory mutation, and persistence.

Current derived spatial supply, economy seasonality, live chain/workplace craft-value resolution, settlement business/labor and abstract storage, and route/cargo transport must be preserved as existing owners. | `mundane_baseline_magic_assisted` | Preferred bounded magic posture | Water-level or soil-moisture indication; frost/storm/pest/disease warning; limited water lift; drainage inspection; protected light; drying assistance; livestock calming/observation; warded store; temporary cool holding | Accepted affinity and vessel/ritual/ward route; finite charge, area/mass/volume, duration, ambient conditions, recharge, skill, installation, maintenance, failure, scarcity, security, institution |
| `parallel_magical_specialty` | Rare specialist lane alongside ordinary production | Small greenhouse-like protected environment, specialist seed vault, rare veterinary observation, dangerous-material containment | Must produce a distinct scarce service without replacing mundane regional agriculture |
| `magic_equivalent_institutional` | Large organized service only if canon later supports it | Guild/temple/academy-maintained warning or ward network, strategic granary protection | Capital/institution scale, fixed infrastructure, expert staff, recharge logistics, security, outage/failure consequences |
| `magic_exclusive` | Presumptively unavailable for ordinary agriculture | Only a future canon-exclusive crop/material/ritual could qualify | Requires explicit canonical source and cannot be inferred from current flora/spells |
| `unstable_or_prohibited` | Reject or mark dangerous | Instant growth, guaranteed rain, automatic reproduction, infinite fertility, universal pest/disease removal, free irrigation, unlimited cold storage, automatic soil recovery | Conflicts with resource, ecology, magic, and gameplay guardrails |

Weather and warning analogues demonstrate why bounded indication is defensible. Agricultural meteorology uses observations, forecasts, and communication for frost, drought, storm, pest, and disease warning, but forecasts are uncertain and depend on observation networks and skilled interpretation ([WMO Guide to Agricultural Meteorological Practices](https://community.wmo.int/site/knowledge-hub/programmes-and-initiatives/agricultural-meteorology/guide-agricultural-meteorological-practices-gamp2010-edition-wmo-no134), B1). Frost protection depends on local meteorology, timing, water/equipment, and maintenance; no warning or intervention guarantees protection ([Snyder, de Melo-Abreu, and Matulich](https://www.fao.org/4/y7223e/y7223e00.pdf), B1). Water lifting remains constrained by head, flow, prime mover, construction, and upkeep even if the prime mover is magical ([Fraenkel](https://www.fao.org/4/ah810e/AH810E00.htm), B1).

Every later magical candidate must answer all of the following before acceptance: canonical affinity; vessel/shard/crystal/cluster/conduit/catalyst/spell/ritual/ward/enchantment route; finite capacity; recharge; target area, mass, or volume; ambient conditions; duration; mundane construction; skill; installation; maintenance; failure mode; scarcity; transport; security; institutional access; why it is not universal; and why it remains attractive to a player. This gate assigns no formulas or vessel capacities.
