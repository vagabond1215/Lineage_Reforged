## 3. Method and source-quality rubric

Research used peer-reviewed primary work, foundational papers, standards, and direct technical publications. Sources were evaluated on:

1. authority and traceability;
2. fit to the exact research question;
3. reproducibility or normative status;
4. transferability without importing setting canon;
5. limitations when moving from ecology, animal behavior, robotics, or formal ontology into a deterministic RPG simulation.

Evidence classes:

- `A1`: normative standard or foundational primary work;
- `A2`: peer-reviewed empirical study, consensus paper, or substantial survey;
- `B1`: direct technical proceedings, official repository, or practitioner publication with explicit limitations;
- `B2`: supporting synthesis used only to clarify a pattern.

External evidence supplies engineering and modeling principles. It is not a vote for elemental lore.

## 6. Owner-graph findings

The minimum conceptual graph is:

```text
authored world/magic/site/entity owners
  -> owner-certified affinity contributions and magical occurrences
  -> affinity observation/aggregation owner
  -> manifestation eligibility and transition owner
  -> persistent entity/presence identity owner

authoritative occurrence + entity perception/recognition
  -> normalized perceived stimulus
  -> temperament prior + target-relative disposition + current condition/memory
  -> capability and action eligibility
  -> deterministic response selection
  -> accepted action/outcome owners
  -> retained elemental interaction evidence
  -> observer projection
  -> narrative realization / Chronicle / Manuscript projections
```

No arrow runs backward from prose, encounter presentation, a hash, or a display alias into authoritative identity or outcome.

The aggregation owner should consume facts from existing owners rather than duplicate climate, terrain, biome, settlement, religion, site, spell, vessel, or encounter authority. Manifestation eligibility is not itself an entity. An entity may later create new contributions, but those are separately accepted effects.

## 7. Environmental affinity-pressure findings

`Affinity pressure` is best treated as a family of owner-produced evidence plus a bounded aggregation/projection, not one universal stored field.

Potential contributors are heterogeneous:

- climate and weather observations;
- terrain, water, geology, vegetation, and biome facts;
- authored sacred, breached, bound, or convergent sites;
- accepted spell, ritual, ward, enchantment, vessel, or magical-infrastructure occurrences;
- settlement activity and remediation;
- already-manifested entities' accepted environmental effects.

Each contribution needs its source owner, locality/extent, element identity after canonical normalization, observation or effective interval, persistence posture, and confidence/authority category. Those conceptual needs do not authorize fields.

A consumer may derive a task-specific pressure view, but the view must not erase the independent contributors. Otherwise correction, explanation, balance, and source removal become impossible.

## 8. Manifestation, locality, source/sink, and threshold findings

Ambient manifestation and discrete entity manifestation should remain separate:

- ambient manifestation is an accepted environmental condition or effect without independent agent identity;
- discrete manifestation creates, attracts, awakens, reveals, or changes a persistent presence/entity through an accepted transition.

Source/sink is useful as a locality analogy: an area may generate or reinforce qualifying conditions, while another area may only retain or receive a presence. Occupancy does not prove local origination.

Thresholds should be transition guards over evidence, not lore truths inferred from one number. Entry may depend on sustained, convergent, or exceptional evidence. Exit may require depletion plus a separate persistence rule. Hysteresis is appropriate only if the later decision explicitly wants history-dependent persistence.

Locality must be explicit enough to distinguish:

- exact object, actor, site, cell, route segment, settlement, habitat, region, or bounded area;
- overlap versus adjacency;
- present observation versus historical residue;
- authoritative extent versus observer-estimated extent.

An entity should persist independently from the source only when its accepted identity/persistence posture permits it. Source removal then changes support, eligibility, or state; it does not silently delete the entity.

## 9. Entity taxonomy and ontology findings

Use orthogonal axes rather than one inheritance ladder.

Likely identity-bearing axes:

- stable entity identity;
- embodiment/substance posture where identity depends on it;
- origin category when it is constitutive rather than merely historical;
- agency and sapience bands when owners need stable behavioral limits;
- persistence mode;
- created, emergent, summoned, awakened, transformed, or born provenance where accepted.

Likely capabilities or relationships:

- perception modes;
- movement and interaction capabilities;
- communication;
- magical compatibility;
- service/binding relationship;
- institutional ownership or allegiance;
- ability to heal, warn, transport, guard, diagnose, provide resources, or alter an environment.

Likely contextual roles:

- guardian;
- territorial occupant;
- escort;
- hunter/predator;
- helper;
- institution-bound protector.

Likely mutable state:

- current disposition toward a target;
- fear, pain, depletion, agitation, satiation, corruption, instability, or dormancy;
- current goal/need;
- selected action;
- familiarity and memory.

`Animal`, `magical animal`, `construct`, `elemental`, `spirit`, and `fae-like` remain candidate descriptive groupings. External ontology research supports separation of axes; it does not validate these names or boundaries.

## 10. Temperament, disposition, and selected-action findings

Maintain four layers:

1. **Temperament prior**: stable authored behavioral tendencies that bias eligibility or selection across repeated contexts.
2. **Disposition**: mutable relation toward a particular actor, group, object, place, activity, or recognized stimulus.
3. **Condition/state**: current physiological, magical, resource, memory, and environmental facts.
4. **Selected response/action**: one accepted next behavior after capability and eligibility checks.

Passive, curious, territorial, cooperative, helpful, assimilative, defensive, predatory, and guardian are not interchangeable:

- passive/curious/defensive can describe current posture;
- territorial and guardian depend on a place, boundary, resource, charge, or relationship;
- cooperative/helpful depend on capability, relationship, motivation, and opportunity;
- predatory depends on resource/target behavior rather than moral alignment;
- assimilative is too ambiguous to accept without later definition of object, mechanism, consent, and outcome.

A stable prior cannot prove current intent. A hostile disposition cannot prove attack. An attack cannot retroactively prove a universally hostile temperament.

## 11. Active/passive magical-stimulus findings

The minimum distinction is between authoritative occurrence, interaction, perception, and recognition.

Active candidate classes:

- cast/channel;
- ritual;
- attack or harmful application;
- healing/restoration;
- ward creation or activation;
- summoning/binding;
- dispelling/suppression;
- deliberate manipulation or transfer.

Passive candidate classes:

- carried or worn enchantment/artifact;
- persistent ward or field;
- residue from an accepted occurrence;
- ambient affinity condition;
- attunement or relationship;
- lineage trait;
- latent capability not currently exercised.

A normalized stimulus needs conceptual identity for source, target/affected area, occurrence class, element/compatibility where known, intensity category, interval, novelty/familiarity basis, ownership/consent facts when relevant, and visibility/perceptibility. These are research needs, not a proposed interface.

Responses based on perception require a supported sense, range/occlusion posture, perceptible cue, and recognition result. Direct magical contact can trigger an authoritative interaction even when the entity did not first perceive or understand the source. Hidden stimulus truth and perceived stimulus must remain separate.

## 12. Response-architecture findings

| Architecture | Determinism/explainability | Authoring/data cost | Persistence/debugging | Foundation-stage fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Closed rules plus explicit state transitions | Strong when ordering, eligibility, and ties are explicit | Low to moderate | Straightforward event/state inspection | Best first posture |
| Behavior tree | Strong if nodes and ordering are deterministic | Moderate; improves modularity at breadth | Requires node/running-state persistence discipline | Later candidate when orchestration grows |
| GOAP | Explainable plans but larger search/state surface | High; needs goals, world model, actions, costs | Replanning and partial-plan identity add burden | Too broad now |
| Utility selection | Deterministic with normalized inputs and ties | Moderate; tuning burden can be high | Must retain candidate scores/reasons | Later ranking layer, not eligibility |
| BDI | Rich separation for deliberative beliefs/goals | Very high | Belief and intention persistence is substantial | Not justified for first slice |
| Hybrid | Can isolate strengths | Highest integration cost | Multiple decision identities and correction paths | Premature |

Recommended smallest safe conceptual posture: a closed, owner-defined response policy with explicit eligibility, deterministic priority/tie handling, a small accepted state-transition model, and retained causal reasons. Optional utility ranking may be evaluated later only among already eligible responses. Behavior trees merit later evaluation if concrete behavior breadth makes closed transition authoring unwieldy. No production library is justified by this research.

## 13. Deterministic response identity and provenance

A replayable response decision must retain or reconstruct:

- normalized stimulus identity and semantic version;
- authoritative context snapshot/reference and event time;
- entity identity and relevant persisted state version;
- behavioral-policy identity/version;
- perception and recognition results;
- candidate response identities;
- eligibility result and rejection reason for material candidates;
- selection ordering, score components if any, and tie-break posture;
- authorized seed/draw identity only where randomness is later accepted;
- selected response identity;
- causal parent occurrence(s);
- accepted action and outcome identities;
- correction/supersession relationship.

The semantic normalizer must decide equivalence before hashing. RFC 8785 can stabilize a JSON byte representation, but it cannot decide whether aliases, omitted defaults, reordered evidence, units, localities, or two event forms mean the same thing.

Determinism means the same accepted initial state, policy/version, normalized inputs, event order, and authorized random identity produce the same selection. It does not require every entity to behave identically.

## 14. Beneficial capability authorization

Helpful outcomes must pass all of these conceptual gates:

1. the entity explicitly possesses the capability;
2. the capability applies to the target/problem;
3. the entity perceives or is directly engaged by a qualifying trigger;
4. disposition, relationship, role, consent, and policy allow consideration;
5. range, access, environment, knowledge, and current condition satisfy eligibility;
6. resource/cost/cooldown or comparable constraints permit use;
7. deterministic response selection chooses it;
8. the owning treatment, magic, travel, quest, economy, inventory, or environmental system accepts the effect;
9. retained evidence links motivation, capability, action, cost, and outcome.

The elemental response owner may request or select a helpful action. It may not manufacture healing, diagnosis, transport, supplies, knowledge, quest completion, or environmental repair outside the relevant owner.

This prevents deus ex machina behavior and makes refusal/failure explainable without exposing hidden facts.

## 15. Warning, escalation, de-escalation, and observer readability

Use an authored graph of eligible postures and actions, not a universal hostility meter or mandatory linear ladder.

Possible readable progression:

```text
presence -> attention -> boundary/warning display -> guarded posture
         -> aid | withdrawal | interception | pursuit | attack | dormancy
```

Transitions may skip stages when an accepted direct attack, breach, binding attempt, or other severe stimulus justifies it. De-escalation can follow withdrawal, consent, restitution, recognized familiarity, changed conditions, or resource relief when policy permits.

Observer-facing evidence should include only perceptible facts: orientation, distance change, visible/audible/magical cue, boundary behavior, accepted action, and observable outcome. Hidden temperament, exact disposition, policy branch, score, unrecognized identity, future action, and motive remain validator/debug facts unless an owner provides a supported disclosure.

Habituation should be stimulus-specific and reversible rather than a global pacification value. Sensitization or renewed escalation likewise needs a causal occurrence.

## 16. Persistence, reinforcement, habituation, decay, migration, dormancy, dissolution, and removal

Retain enough information to distinguish:

- why and where the entity/presence originated or arrived;
- whether it requires continuing support;
- which accepted occurrences reinforced, depleted, displaced, bound, or transformed it;
- current locality and movement/migration cause;
- relevant familiarity/memory and its scope;
- current active, dormant, dispersed, dissolving, dead, removed, or corrected posture;
- last transition, causal evidence, and policy/version;
- recoverable versus irreversible outcomes.

Permissible conceptual outcomes:

- **migration/displacement**: the same entity identity changes locality through accepted movement;
- **dormancy**: identity persists while activity/perception/action is suspended or bounded;
- **dispersal**: a coherent presence loses local concentration, with identity consequences left to canon;
- **dissolution**: an in-world terminal or transformative outcome accepted by its owner;
- **death/destruction**: an upstream consequence owner accepts the terminal outcome;
- **runtime unloading/despawn**: presentation/runtime optimization only; it must not assert an in-world outcome;
- **correction/removal**: invalid or superseded state is corrected with provenance, not narrated as death or dissolution.

Reinforcement should be a new accepted environmental/entity effect, not circular proof that the entity must exist. Anti-farming requires separate economy/loot/reappearance authority: unloading/reloading, repeated stimulus, or source recreation must not duplicate identity, drops, favorability, or consequences.

## 17. Narrative and consumer integration

Elemental owners decide:

- manifestation and identity;
- authoritative stimulus and interaction;
- perception and recognition;
- temperament/disposition/current condition inputs;
- candidates, eligibility, selected response, action request, and accepted outcome.

Elemental interaction evidence records exact event-time facts, provenance, relevant policy identity, selected response, accepted action/outcome, and observer-safe projection inputs. The accepted narrative pipeline then:

1. validates owner-certified evidence;
2. projects facts for a specific observer;
3. realizes deterministic text;
4. validates the realization;
5. falls back without inventing facts.

Narration may say an observable presence brightened, recoiled, blocked a ford, issued a perceivable warning, moved closer, attacked, aided, withdrew, or became still when those facts are accepted. It may not infer hatred, gratitude, guardianship, sapience, intent, future action, elemental identity, or hidden capability merely from presentation.

Chronicle and Manuscript remain projections. They cannot become the source for affinity, identity, disposition, or outcome on reload.

## 18. Directly transferable principles

- Preserve heterogeneous owner evidence; do not collapse world and magic facts into one canonical scalar.
- Separate source, observation/field view, manifestation transition, persistent entity, disposition, response, and outcome.
- Separate stable identity/type from role, relationship, capability, mutable state, and selected action.
- Separate temperament prior, target-relative disposition, condition, and action.
- Capability and preconditions determine eligibility before priority or utility.
- Perceived facts differ from authoritative hidden facts.
- Retain policy, candidates, eligibility, selection reason, and causal parents for explainability.
- History-dependent transitions require retained history.
- Runtime removal is not an in-world outcome.
- Narrative consumes accepted evidence and cannot decide elemental facts.

## 19. Adaptable patterns

- Source/sink reasoning for production versus occupancy and migration.
- Threshold entry with potentially different persistence/exit conditions.
- Entity-driven environmental feedback recorded as new accepted contributions.
- Stimulus-specific habituation, spontaneous recovery, and sensitization.
- Contextual territoriality tied to boundaries/resources rather than moral alignment.
- Readable threat and de-escalation cues without exposing hidden decision state.
- A small closed response policy now, with utility ranking or behavior-tree orchestration considered only after demonstrated need.
- Event source/ID and provenance relationships adapted to local deterministic saves.

## 20. Dependency candidates for later evaluation

No new production dependency is recommended now.

Later bounded evaluation candidates:

- a behavior-tree approach, only if accepted behavior breadth and interruptibility exceed a closed response policy;
- a lightweight ontology/vocabulary validation approach, only if entity axes and aliases become too complex for existing schema/semantic validators;
- canonical-serialization support, only after semantic normalization and fingerprint consumers are accepted.

GOAP, BDI, general planners, full W3C PROV, and external ontology engines are not justified for the first elemental slice.

## 21. Cautions and anti-patterns

- One `affinityPressure` number becoming the owner of climate, geography, magic, and site truth.
- A `hostility` number driving all warnings, attacks, aid, and relationships.
- Species/class tags used as current intent.
- `guardian`, `territorial`, `helpful`, or `predatory` treated as substance.
- Passive enchantment presence treated as deliberate casting.
- Hidden authoritative stimulus automatically treated as perceived.
- Utility scores bypassing capability or preconditions.
- Random selection without retained seed/draw identity and deterministic ties.
- Hashes standing in for semantic normalization.
- Despawn used as dissolution, death, source depletion, or reward reset.
- An entity's existence circularly proving the field that creates it.
- Helpful magic bypassing treatment, quest, route, economy, inventory, consent, cost, or consequence owners.
- Current River/Storm examples generalized into universal elemental hostility.
- Ecological metaphors imported as literal setting biology.
- External mythology or another game's behavior treated as canon.

1. **Minimum owner graph:** authored world/magic owners -> affinity contributions -> aggregation/manifestation eligibility -> persistent entity -> perceived stimulus -> state/capability eligibility -> deterministic response -> accepted upstream outcome -> evidence -> observer narrative.
2. **Affinity pressure posture:** a family of owner-produced evidence with task-specific bounded aggregation/projection, not one universal field.
3. **Separation:** source owns a contribution; field/observation is a locality-and-time view; manifestation is an accepted transition; entity owns persistent identity; disposition is mutable and target-relative.
4. **Taxonomy axes:** stable identity, constitutive embodiment/origin, agency/sapience limits, and persistence may be identity; guardian/service/territorial relations are roles or relationships; helpful/predatory/defensive postures and current condition are mutable; actions are occurrences.
5. **Temperament versus disposition/action:** temperament is a repeated-context prior, disposition concerns a particular target/context, condition supplies current constraints, and selected action is one policy result.
6. **Stimulus distinctions required now:** active versus passive; authoritative occurrence versus perceived cue; source/target/locality; direct interaction versus observation; duration; novelty/familiarity; compatibility; ownership/consent; visibility/recognition.
7. **Smallest safe response architecture:** closed deterministic eligibility rules plus a small explicit state-transition policy and causal reasons.
8. **Determinism/explainability:** version normalized inputs, state and policy; retain candidates, eligibility/rejections, ordering/ties, authorized randomness, selection, causes, and outcomes.
9. **Readable escalation:** expose perceptible staged cues and accepted actions; keep exact hidden state and future intent private; do not require one universal ladder.
10. **Beneficial behavior:** require explicit capability, applicable target, perceived/direct trigger, relationship/consent, access/range/knowledge, resources/cost, deterministic selection, and upstream effect acceptance.
11. **Persistence information:** origin/arrival, support dependency, locality, identity, accepted reinforcements/depletions, memory scope, current posture, last transition/cause, policy version, and recoverability.
12. **Disperse/dissolve/migrate/dormant/remove:** only through an entity/presence owner's accepted transition; runtime unloading is not such a transition, and correction must retain supersession provenance.
13. **Narrative evidence:** accepted identity only when observer-safe, perceptible cues, selected/accepted action, outcome, locality/time, and provenance. Hidden temperament, disposition, candidates, scores, policy branches, unrecognized identities, and future outcomes remain hidden/debug facts.
14. **Dependencies:** none now; behavior trees, vocabulary tooling, or canonical serialization merit later bounded evaluation only after concrete accepted need.
15. **Remaining setting canon:** Light/Darkness grouping, final aliases, environmental mappings, taxonomy names/boundaries, temperament distributions, perception rules, stimulus compatibility, behavior policies, capability grants, persistence meanings, and all formulas/content.
16. **Further broad research:** no. The durable decision can proceed. A focused follow-up should occur only if it selects an architecture or ontology requirement that needs implementation-specific evaluation.
