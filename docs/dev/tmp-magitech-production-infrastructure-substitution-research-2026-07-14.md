## 1. Gate Result

Gate 7 passes as a complete research gate. The strongest repository finding is that magic is neither wholly inert nor a production system. The live civilization path deterministically derives three crystal reserves and four magic-service availability rows for each of 88 settlements, and a narrow combat path recognizes 11 spell-resolution hooks. At the same time, no reviewed owner executes the production, preservation, pumping, heating, cooling, lighting, navigation, warding, field-healing, affinity-binding, or civic-service use cases named in `world.magic_infrastructure`; no owner consumes or recharges a settlement reserve through physical work; and the generic known-spell resolver remains an inert planning envelope. Static compatibility, derived availability, and effect execution must remain separate.

The exact stone/earth relationship is one affinity expressed through multiple owner vocabularies, not two independent affinities. Crystal IDs, religion, and settlement presentation use `stone`; crystal `affinityKey`, spells, magical-material gemstone bias, and the combat framework use `earth`; the global combat rule explicitly normalizes legacy `stone` to `earth`. Magic-service boundary fields currently admit both strings. Integration should preserve the current records while making this translation explicit; it should not create an earth crystal or a second stone spell family.

The strongest accepted principles are:

1. Ordinary technology remains the baseline for every one of the 43 inherited physical demands and all 11 contexts.
2. Magic candidates must identify one or more of 12 distinct postures: creation, extraction, movement, concentration, conversion, transfer, storage, release, stabilization, measurement, warning, or containment.
3. Creation of matter or energy remains unsupported unless a later explicit repository authority establishes it. Current water, fire, ice, wind, thunder, stone/earth, light, darkness, and neutral metadata does not.
4. A useful magical service remains finite and housed: a vessel, charge, recharge route, compatible mundane enclosure and transmission path, specialist labor, inspection, maintenance, observable failure, scarcity, institutional access, and ordinary fallback are all required.
5. Current single-affinity records do not establish compound vessels, clean multi-affinity combination, shared recharge, combined capacity, or stability bonuses. The conservative research posture is two independently recharged vessels in mundane compound housing, with neutral regulation unresolved.
6. Existing providers and assets should be reused before new records. The four magic-service records, 18 guilds, six religious orders, 22 buildings, 58 workplaces, 110 job IDs, four unhosted building templates, and existing conduit/focus/vessel items provide more authority than a new generic magic shop, guild, or tower.
7. Player value comes from bounded exceptions, reliability choices, portable or strategic access, institutional competition, repair and recharge logistics, and visible failure—not from bypassing the ordinary economy.

The strongest rejected claims are free matter; free or perpetual energy; water affinity automatically creating potable water; fire replacing fuel, refractory housing, or exhaust; ice guaranteeing preservation or sanitation; wind supplying unlimited motive force; thunder implying a modern grid; light sterilizing; darkness removing heat or matter; stone/earth creating construction material; flawless wards; automatic diagnosis or healing; lossless production; universal transport or communication; exact unsupported throughput, duration, temperature, pressure, range, or capacity; and duplicate elemental providers added only for flavor.

This artifact authorizes no repository change. The next route is `Cross-Domain Natural Resources, Materials, Production, And Magitech Research Integration`. Revised `Version 0.6.5 - Item, Material, And Recipe Static Content Expansion` remains blocked.

## 6. Conservation And Transformation Posture

External evidence establishes the ordinary baseline: heat and work change system energy; cooling moves heat to a sink; water and steam behavior is state-dependent; and ordinary water occupies stores and moves through fluxes and phase changes ([EXT-01](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/heat-transfer-3/), [EXT-02](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2022/06/spotlight-overhauling-refrigeration-industry-fight-global-warming), [EXT-04](https://www.nist.gov/srd/nistir-5078), [EXT-12](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-earths-water-cycle)). External physics does not define fantasy canon, but current repository canon supplies no contrary creation authority.

| Posture | Required Gate 7 meaning | Default disposition when authority is absent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `creation` | Matter or energy appears from an explicitly owned source | `canonically_unsupported`; reject free water, heat, cold, air, stone, charge, or light |
| `extraction` | Existing matter is separated from a source or reservoir | Require source, access, residue, depletion/ecology question, tools, and receiver; defer execution |
| `movement` | Existing matter changes position | Require path, head/load, losses, destination, spill/backflow, and finite work |
| `concentration` | Matter, energy, or information becomes denser or more localized | Require rejected fraction, heat/waste, containment, and failure; no lossless focusing |
| `conversion` | Existing energy or matter changes form | Account for input, output, losses, byproducts, and compatible equipment |
| `transfer` | Heat, force, matter, or information crosses a boundary | Require source and sink, coupling, insulation/seals, leakage, and finite rate |
| `storage` | Matter or energy remains available over time | Require finite capacity, housing, leakage/decay, security, recharge/replenishment, and inspection |
| `release` | Stored matter or energy becomes available | Require a controlled path, guards, rate/pressure question, residual state, and failure |
| `stabilization` | Variation is bounded around an existing condition | Does not create equilibrium, repair damage, or eliminate maintenance; require reference and fallback |
| `measurement` | A specified property is observed against a reference | Does not cause control, diagnosis, purification, or perfect accuracy; require calibration and interpretation |
| `warning` | A threshold or change produces an indication | Does not prevent the event; require false-positive/negative, range, relay, acknowledgement, and action route |
| `containment` | Matter, energy, or access remains within a boundary | Require mundane load path, material compatibility, seals, vent/drain/release, inspection, and bypass/failure |

Every later candidate applies these postures. `measurement != control`; `warning != prevention`; `stabilization != permanent maintenance-free operation`; `containment != flawless barrier`; `cooling != preservation`; `airflow != motive power`; `ignition != continuing heat`; and water movement, lifting, condensation, separation, purification, storage, and creation remain distinct.
