The decision must include:

### 1. Live Authority Baseline

Record exact relevant current files, types, state, presentation components, view models, labels, warnings, notifications, Chronicle output, observer-like data, and absence/presence of crisis behavior.

### 2. Observer, Assessment, Urgency, And Presentation Owners

Define separate conceptual owners for:

- viewpoint/observer identity;
- visibility and access;
- direct observation;
- attributed patient/helper/provider report;
- trained assessment;
- magical sensing;
- diagnosis confidence/knowledge provenance;
- qualitative urgency;
- renderer-safe projection;

- validator-only evidence;
- UI, narrative, dialogue, and Chronicle consumers.

No renderer, UI, Chronicle, or crisis orchestrator may own health truth, diagnosis, process mutation, care effect, death, or future outcome.

### 3. Evidence Classes

Decide exact conceptual claim classes for:

- directly perceived;
- recognized under accepted authority;
- inferred under a named accepted assessment rule;
- attributed report;
- trained qualified judgment;
- magically sensed qualified judgment;
- unknown, withheld, or unsafe to expose.

Keep source identity, time, observer, capability, confidence, and correction provenance conceptually available without accepting schema fields unless a later package is ready.

### 4. Assessment Request, Admission, And Result

Apply the accepted occurrence taxonomy to an assessment:

- request identity and normalized intent;
- observer/assessor, patient, target concern, access, capability, equipment, environment, and consent evidence;
- pre-admission rejection;
- admitted assessment occurrence;
- deterministic or named-channel uncertain accepted result;
- no-result posture;
- owner-specific receipts or projections;
- retry, duplicate delivery, replay, and correction.

An assessment result reports capability-bounded knowledge. It never mutates the process being assessed.

### 5. Hidden Truth And Diagnosis Boundary

Define how hidden process identity, stage, severity, source, timing, probability, prognosis, future outcome, and validator evidence remain private.

No one visible sign, HP value, status label, role, item, spell, service, morality, religion, or prose line may establish diagnosis.

Exact diagnosis appears only when an upstream owner accepts that the observer knows it through applicable capability, access, examination, and result evidence.

### 6. Qualitative Urgency And Care-Need Projection

Define qualitative urgency as an upstream owner-approved projection over accepted facts, never a renderer calculation from hidden numbers.

Separate:

- visible concern;
- qualified urgency;
- immediate threat;
- stable for current conditions;
- unresolved care need;
- destination/provider need;
- functional limitation;
- actual death or restoration eligibility.

Do not define numeric tiers, timers, probabilities, thresholds, colors, alerts, or UI behavior unless an exact later package is justified.

### 7. First-Scope Projection Compatibility

Disposition observer-safe evidence for:

- external hemorrhage;
- confirmed internal hemorrhage versus suspicion;
- airway obstruction;
- post-submersion respiratory compromise;
- systemic hypothermia;
- hot-altered heat crisis.

Also preserve the boundaries for shock-like circulatory deterioration, poison-family gaps, local freezing injury, contextual heat illness, superficial/serious burns, and chemical/electrical/inhalation mechanisms.

Do not author diagnostic criteria, symptoms as guaranteed proof, treatment instructions, or final strings.

### 8. Care Attempt, Process Effect, Transport, And Destination Projection

Separate presentation of:

- care requested;
- attempt admitted/rejected;
- visible attempt occurrence;
- accepted care result;
- process-owner effect receipt;
- supportive result without process control;
- stabilization or suppression under conditions;
- unresolved or definitive-care need;
- extraction/transport;
- destination offer, availability, access, arrival, admission, and provider result.

Never present one step as proof of the next.

### 9. Reassessment And Temporal Truth

Reassessment is a new accepted result with new evidence and confidence. It must not rewrite what an earlier observer knew.

Address owner-certified movement, delay, environment, observed change, intervention response, destination/capability, and upstream-state triggers.

Preserve event-time facts, chronology, simultaneity, correction, and earlier uncertainty.

### 10. Trained And Magical Knowledge

Decide how ordinary observers, helpers, trained healers, alchemists, scholars, and magical diagnosticians may know different facts.

Capability, access, examination, equipment, and accepted result must bound specificity and confidence.

Magic may extend observation or communication only through an explicit magic-owner result. It must not:

- provide omniscient diagnosis by default;
- expose hidden validator evidence;
- guarantee prognosis;
- collapse observation into process truth;
- justify modern-scientific exposition or pseudo-scientific technobabble.

### 11. Renderer-Safe And Validator-Only Separation

Define two closed conceptual channels:

- renderer-safe claims available to scene planning, deterministic realization, UI, dialogue, narrative, and Chronicle;
- validator-only evidence used only to reject leakage or contradiction.

Validator-only evidence must not be available to lexical choice, scene planning, optional request construction, telemetry, logs, UI, or player-facing reports.

If separation cannot be verified, presentation receives only the smallest renderer-safe subset.

### 12. Player-Facing Language

Preserve mandatory constraints:

- internal ids and contracts may be precise and hidden;
- player-facing language must be brief, concrete, everyday, and setting-appropriate;
- prefer observable or attributed wording such as `bleeding badly`, `struggling to breathe`, `faint and cold`, `burning hot and confused`, `the bleeding has slowed`, `stable for now`, `needs a healer`, or `must be taken somewhere safer`;
- avoid ordinary display use of `hypovolemic`, `respiratory compromise`, `cyanosis`, `perfusion`, `neurological deficit`, `syndrome`, or `triage`;
- distinguish ordinary observation, trained judgment, and magical sensing;
- express uncertainty plainly;
- do not mechanically expose internal ids.

Do not author a final copy catalog.

### 13. Deterministic Realization And Fallback

- closed renderer-safe fact input;

- source and semantic version provenance;
