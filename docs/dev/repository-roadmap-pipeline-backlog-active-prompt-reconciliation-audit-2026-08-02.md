## Source, Caller, Persistence, And Assertion Findings

The current production surface confirms the prompt rather than superseding it:

- `normal-defeat.ts` validates the explicit pending destination, but immediate `resolveDestination(...)` still accepts current/campaign-start strings without the same exact known-safe-settlement proof and has no `sole_known_settlement` receipt source.
- `campaign-session.ts` recognizes same-session duplicates from retained in-memory mutation results, but a restarted session cannot prove the completed repair from a distinct durable completion-continuity identity.
- `NormalDefeatReceiptState` has original defeat continuity only; the accepted optional `recoveryCompletionContinuityId` field is not yet implemented.
- pending repair validates receipt/ledger/Chronicle/notification presence, but does not yet validate the full original HP, Stamina, MP, clock, captured tick, total play tick, and accepted-ledger-tick provenance before mutation.
- `saveManager.ts` and `App.tsx` are real publication, migration, retry, load, recovery, and account-consumer callers; a lower-level-only patch would be insufficient.
- the focused persistence suite proves current nonterminal/pending behavior, reachable repair, in-session duplicate handling, publication recovery, migration, and account consumers, but lacks the full new restart, provenance, and immediate-destination assertions required by `0.6.9.7`.
- TS implementation files remain source authority; one-line `.js` public mirrors and `index.ts`/`index.js` exports require exact mirror verification during implementation.
