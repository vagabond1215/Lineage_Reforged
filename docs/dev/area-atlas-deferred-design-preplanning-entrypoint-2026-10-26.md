## Today's actionable decision

**Area, not Home and not old World:** default when no nav is selected; actual current place heading, environmental/observed context, real legal actions, Travel, Activity/Quest/Combat priority, true bounded recent results, shortcuts. No six-item World submenu. **Codex Atlas** owns non-immediate geographic reference, known places and wider map presentation. Current World synthetic map pins must not be promoted as physical coordinates.

**Next consumer order:**
- Independent PR #19–#23 review and real gear/Save/Echo/Dev acceptance, then source-confirmed Area/Atlas UI work.
- A selected logged-in barebones Soundings/encounter/restart acceptance test.
- A single exact resource-instance and attempt/physical-output loop with known good item owner; no general economy.
- One later genuinely needed domain consumer, e.g. present NPC/provider, typed environmental scenario, or one descendant-family unlock.

**Narrative posture:** Existing accepted names stay intact; proposed Area copy and future location/character names go through parent geography/culture/economy/history, uniqueness, observer-knowledge and source-provenance checks. No cookie-cutter generator, fictional current NPCs, magical environmental claims or invented maps. New authoring remains separate from this planning pass.
