# Dungeon Core Mana Refinement, Crystalline Facets and Consciousness Threads

Date: 2026-10-29 (America/New_York).
Status: **user-directed provisional Dungeon Core design, documentation only; NOT executable gameplay authority**.
Scope: held Lineage: Reforged design PR #46, `HOLD_NAMED_CONSUMER`, on its isolated connector branch. Does not change master, selected game, schemas, saves, actual creature/Dungeon records or Game version.

## 1. Source scale and the ordinary leyline relationship

**Accepted user direction:** a Dungeon Core is tiny compared with the large magical source systems that feed the deep leylines. Its ordinary absorption is negligible relative to the whole and should **not** be treated as draining, relocating, destroying, poisoning or meaningfully weakening a major leyline or its constituent elemental identity. Its physical size is not a license for unbounded mana income: the Core is limited chiefly by how much mana it can legitimately access, absorb, stabilize, store, expend and refine at any moment.

A Core can have **net positive** ordinary mana economics without retaining all incoming energy. Dungeon existence and operations may include emitted or exuded mana, ordinary leakage/loss, ambient release, costs of maintaining effects, directed magical work, excavation/shaping, creature support, research or other accepted processes. Distinguish **gross source potential**, **actual incoming capture**, **expenditure**, **environmental return/loss**, **currently usable unrefined/stabilized reserve**, and **permanently integrated structural refinement**. Not all lost or emitted mana is destroyed; its eventual fate belongs to the world's magic/environment owners.

Convergence access can be strategically transformative for a weak or growing Core **without** being globally significant for the line. A Core reaching a verified powerful node may be **capture-bound, storage-bound or refinement/attention-bound**. Because the Core can only consolidate so much at a time, proximity to near-limitless external flow does not confer limitless core power, immediate advancement, free monsters, unlimited magical tasks, automatic element mastery or a changed city environment. It may still be an excellent reason for a Dungeon to expand toward a city-centred convergence.

**Exceptional intervention remains unresolved:** future rare, enormous-scale magical/geophysical events might affect local exposure or a deep line only under separate certified owner rules. Ordinary accumulation/absorption, and even a prosperous Dungeon, do not meet that exception. Do not silently invent depletion thresholds from relative source scale.

## 2. Core refinement as deliberate geometric crystallization

**Accepted metaphor and proposed physical/magical model:** loose charcoal powder is not a finished diamond. In the same way, a Core containing large amounts of raw or usable mana has not necessarily incorporated it into durable, well-organized **Core structure**. Advancement requires deliberate compression, shaping, stabilization and correction of **imperfections, misalignment and asymmetry**. The result is a coherent, increasingly refined **faceted sphere** of densely ordered magical/crystalline structure.

This is a fantastical analogy; **no claim of real carbon-to-diamond manufacturing, literal ordinary mineral chemistry or actual equal-density material construction** is made. The fantasy Core's inner architecture and its facets are real world-design concepts, but exact physical appearance/geometry and visual revelation require later owners.

Suggested causal sequence (not a selected formula):

```text
source-backed environmental mana
→ sensed / accessible source relation
→ captured, stabilized usable Core reserve (bounded)
→ mana budget reserved + purposeful active refinement and time
→ compression / shaping / symmetry alignment / flaw resolution
→ durable, structurally integrated crystalline layer
→ repeat refinement onto the prepared layer
→ qualitative Rank/major breakthrough may reorganize facet topology
```

Completing one structurally sound layer enables **another layer of the same Core**, not a new Core, child/soul, free resource spawn or automatically a new Rank. A new layer can be larger, denser, stronger or more exacting according to future authoring. A Core may have unintegrated reserves while refinements are incomplete, or a beautifully refined geometry while external capture is temporarily poor.

**Flaws and symmetry:** quality of shape, alignment and integration is distinct from total mana and number of facets. Mistakes might cause inefficiency, constrained throughput, instability, wasted energy, longer repair/realignment, or practical reduction in available attention *only when* a later safe owner selects such consequences. No unavoidable shattering/death, arbitrary random punishment, Star loss, permanent defects or forced restart is approved. Refined historical structure and already committed events survive save/load and longer world-time progression; failed or incomplete work does not duplicate the committed mana.

**Layer versus breakthrough:** ordinary layer work and intrarank cultivation primarily improve structural integrity, throughput, efficiency, control, refinement and quality. Major Rank/breakthrough changes may **reconfigure the number, geometry, and potential complexity of facets**, allowing a new level of parallel awareness. Do not infer one new facet, one new shell, one Star, one Rank or one task per unit of mana. Exact progression boundaries are unselected.

## 3. Six-faced beginning, finer facets, increasing spherical efficiency

**Accepted directional shape:** the least efficient primitive reference is a **six-faced cube**, where few broad faces and pronounced corners represent comparatively crude distribution of Core power and attention. As the Core advances through deliberate successful refinements and topology-changing breakthroughs, it can become more finely faceted and **closer to spherical**, signifying better overall structural refinement, even coverage, energy-control efficiency and attention partitioning.

**Important separations:**

- **Facet count** = the number of genuinely stable, owner-certified faces/regions capable of supporting conscious processing threads in the magical architecture. Quality matters: nominal faces, tiny imperfections, visual glints and shell growth do not automatically count as new functional facets.
- **Sphericity/symmetry** = how evenly and coherently the Core is shaped. More faces are usually the route toward a smoother, more spherical Core **when properly aligned**, but arbitrary extra jagged faces are not inherently more efficient. A poorly formed high-face-count shape can perform worse than an orderly lower-count one until refined.
- **Layer count / structural consolidation** = how much mana has been deliberately integrated into enduring coherent structure; not the current spendable reserve.
- **Rank/breakthrough** = authorization for new structural topology and qualitative capability, not just total reserves or visible growth.
- **Active thread capacity** = how many potential facet-generated conscious threads are **stable, awake, coordinated and permitted to perform tasks** at a given time, subject to Rank/maturity, attention, knowledge, world-time and any applicable active-work cap.

Do not naively use `active threads = 6` for the first cube. **Each stable facet can produce/support a potential consciousness thread**, but the Core may initially be able to activate or coordinate **fewer than all its potential facets**. This accommodates very low early parallel Task capacity and a six-faced primitive structure. Later breakthroughs can grow both possible facet count and practical coordinated throughput. Any eventual exact one-to-one *active* thread rule must reconcile starting shape, maturation and future task caps explicitly.

No canonical polyhedron sequence (cube → octahedron → dodecahedron etc.), number of faces for any Rank, angular geometry, maximum facets, facet density metric, sphericity equation, mana-compression ratio, visual shader or numeric efficiency function is chosen. Spherical convergence should not imply literally unlimited/infinitely small faces or infinite tasks.

## 4. Threads of consciousness and actually doing work

**Accepted user direction:** each sufficiently formed facet is capable of producing a **thread of consciousness**, letting one Core **split its attention** and perform multiple real tasks concurrently. These threads are coordinated aspects of **one Core identity**, not automatically autonomous new people, Dungeon sub-Cores, souls, separate guild actors, separate saves, independent remote agents or unrestricted game AI.

A facet-thread can potentially contribute to attention, sensing, manipulation, refinement, dungeon management, direct magical work or coordination. Actual Task eligibility must separately check permitted capability/skill, available mana, tools/materials, physical reach, labor, active thread capacity, safe synchronization, target legitimacy and World Time.

Complex transformative work may occupy **several synchronized threads** rather than multiplying output for free. Other passive phenomena may operate without consuming a full active thread when specifically owned and accounted for. Most importantly:

```text
many potential facets
≠ many activated concurrent threads
≠ unlimited independent decisions
≠ sufficient mana / knowledge / physical access
≠ an ability to complete all tasks instantly
```

A newer layer should not automatically increase thread count without an appropriately matured or reorganized facet topology. An unrelated magic supply does not increase concurrent attention unless the Core actually progresses. A Core with abundant energy but low activated attention can still be strategically constrained.

**Dungeon-only reference inspection (read-only):** `vagabond1215/Dungeon` `main`, `docs/design/CORE_WORK_THREADS_CONCURRENT_MANIPULATION.md`, `CORE_PROGRESSION.md`, `CORE_ABILITIES.md`, and `MAGIC_AND_ESSENCE.md`. That project distinguishes usable essence, structural essence, progress proofs and task concurrency; its current rank-banded working task-capacity example is Iron=2, Bronze=4, Silver=8, Gold=16, Platinum=32, Diamond=64, Mythril=128, with Stars generally improving thread quality rather than number. **These are useful compatibility observations, not approved Lineage numerical ranks/caps/formulas.** The six-faced visualization must not silently overwrite a preexisting two-active-task reference, nor does that reference force a six-faced Core to possess only two actual facets.

No task automation, permissions, combat, AI, activity execution or background scheduler is authorized by this document.

## 5. World effects, dungeon ambitions and character continuity

- **Convergence capture:** a Core can deliberately pursue a valuable field. Its evolving faceted structure determines how effectively it can actually exploit an accessible source rather than granting a new mana-generating leyline. Multiple dungeons cannot double-count one external flow as infinitely duplicated source value.
- **Dungeon ecology:** actual committed use/exudation/loss could eventually contribute to local magical conditions and dungeon theme if the magic/environment owner certifies effects. It must not automatically spawn monsters, poison a forest, change a city climate or create a free dungeon-wide buff.
- **Metropolis conflict:** even a less-developed Core could see an enormous strategic motive in a city convergence while having insufficient capture, refinement or threads to make immediate use of it. Civic resistance, wards, excavated access, legal arrangements and the city's own history still govern what can happen.
- **World timescales:** refining each new layer, reorganizing breakthroughs, pursuing a leyline and building tunnels cost World Time. Live/Attended clock mode and future queued actions do not give a Core infinite idle tasks or free progress; actual active owner-backed work and causal sources are necessary.
- **Causality and history:** older refinements and completed tasks have retained evidence, source, commitment and time. Generation/era advancement does not reroll facet topology, mana deposits, historical structural milestones, city breach history or identity.
- **Metaphysical boundary:** no implied ninth canonical element, soul requirement, reincarnation outcome, morality, claim of deity, or supernatural powers beyond a Core's accepted capabilities. The eight intrinsic elements in Lineage remain unchanged.

## 6. Suggested future owner-separated records (no schema approved)

- Magic geography/source: actual leyline location, external source magnitude, background stability and separately certified local magical emissions, never Core-owned hidden world truth.
- Dungeon Core current instance: stable identity; Rank/breakthrough proof; refined-layer history/quality; real facet topology and coherent facet condition; potential vs active threads; owner-backed mana/reserve/structural accounting; learning/skills/knowledge; lawful reach and work history.
- Task execution: authorized task, required threads (including synchronization), physical/time/resource commitment, actual accepted outcomes.
- World/environment: validated energy emissions and feedback that can affect local manifestation; independent from religious interpretation and ordinary natural habitats.
- Observer projection: hidden core internal facet geometry only visible to those with legitimate senses, reports or approved observation; no omniscient city knowledge.

Suggested user-facing visual concepts after an owner selects a UI: a crude six-faced crystalline Core gradually becomes a finely faceted sphere with visible integrated growth layers, with facet activity corresponding to concurrent attention. This is a **future concept**, not an image/3D-asset generation request or current UI change.

## 7. Prospective cross-owner acceptance tests (not executed)

- CF-01: an ordinary Core can absorb mana from an accessible node without moving, deleting or measurably destabilizing the immense deep leyline.
- CF-02: captured mana is bounded separately from global source potential, local accessibility, efficiency, storage, active thread capacity and conversion into structural refinement.
- CF-03: usage, expenditure, leakage and emission can coexist with positive net reserve growth without treating all input as permanent profit.
- CF-04: spent/returned/lost mana is not simultaneously kept as unspent reserve or duplicated structural energy.
- CF-05: an accessible powerful convergence does not bypass a weak Core's capture/refinement/attention limits.
- CF-06: large unrefined reserve does not advance Rank or automatically create a new stable layer.
- CF-07: committed active refinement requires legitimate mana, time, eligible capability, shaping/symmetry effort and appropriate task capacity.
- CF-08: unfinished/misaligned refinement does not silently duplicate committed input, free work or force an unselected terminal/Core-death outcome.
- CF-09: a completed layer adds to the **same persistent Core** without creating another independent Core/identity.
- CF-10: Rank/breakthrough may permit facet-topology reconfiguration, while ordinary layer growth alone does not automatically add a facet/task.
- CF-11: a crude six-faced reference is less efficient than a properly consolidated higher-facet near-spherical Core, with no exact formula implied.
- CF-12: nominal face count alone cannot beat severe asymmetry or bypass quality/maturity/synchronization requirements.
- CF-13: each stable certified facet can support a potential consciousness thread, but a six-facet early Core need not run six simultaneous tasks.
- CF-14: potential threads, awake/activated threads, authorized active Task slots and actually executing work remain distinct and bounded.
- CF-15: concurrent complex refinement may require synchronized attention; two declared threads do not make a job take zero time.
- CF-16: a thread remains an aspect of a single Core, not an automatic new person, soul, autonomous dangerous AI or disconnected sub-Core.
- CF-17: spare external mana does not invent knowledge, mana-using species capability, remote sense or physical passage.
- CF-18: committed emissions may influence local conditions only through an accepted world/environment effect; no automatic natural/dungeon ecology rewrite.
- CF-19: collapse/closure of a physical capture connection stops legitimate new Core income while the leyline still exists.
- CF-20: rank labels, core shapes and external Dungeon-reference task counts are not imported as approved Lineage numeric balance.
- CF-21: world-time and long-era continuation preserve earlier refined layers, capacities, legitimate tasks and their historical causes.
- CF-22: public observers cannot read hidden facet topology, current reserves or intent merely from a nearby magical field.
