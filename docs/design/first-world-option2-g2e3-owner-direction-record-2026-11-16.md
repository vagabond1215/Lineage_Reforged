### Valtherion V1: low-tide tidepool opportunity, not a resource spawn

The owner considers V1's exposed low-tide margins potentially valuable as **rare cold-water tidepool harvesting terrain**, with example concepts including specialty pearls/coral and premium seafood/shellfish. This establishes **candidate creative direction only**. Before any ecological or economic activation, independently validate water temperature, real habitat plausibility, protected species/rarity, actual harvest access, local tide timing, risk, gathering balance, legal control and ecology/resource catalogs. Do not auto-place any pearl bed, coral, shellfish or fish resource from a rendered mask. Any future tide-dependent foot connection must be explicit, time-gated, evaluated for passability and independently tested before the travel graph changes; ordinary wagon/cart/caravan access stays disallowed under this design direction.

### What the acceptance does *not* do

These owner responses remove the **nine pending preference choices** that prevented directional coastline authoring. They do **not** close the distinct technical/acceptance gates: original 16K source topology, independently certified whole-feature contours, holes, closure/winding, cross-window overlap and seam consistency, four-/eight-neighbor and erosion sensitivity, registered pixel-center 16K→2048 mapping, stable physical-component IDs, named child-region polygons, political or maritime sovereignty, tides/route permission, source Voronoi, biome/relief/ecology truth, save migration or runtime publication.
