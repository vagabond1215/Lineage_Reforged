## Owner-contract boundaries

- **Identity:** `getActionTemplate` keeps exact basic melee separate from item grants, while item-granted profiles carry action type, source type/source ID and item ID. `resolveActionTypeFamily` is an alias for effects/skills, not command identity. The nearby ledger currently records exact action type and target, not a distinct item instance; equipment changes during an encounter need their own decision.
- **Legality:** WorldPanel reads owner-projected commands, and the nearby owner recomputes the legal action/target after exact source snapshot, revision, encounter, actor and tick checks. The projection currently selects living enemy IDs and damage hooks only. Engine `queueManualCombatCommand` itself is a lower-level mutator that trusts a caller-supplied source; do not treat direct queue fixture success as player authorization. The first slice should share one narrow self-target Block legality rule across projection and revalidation, with no friendly damage or invented spells.
- **Execution:** A legal command is queued; the accepted step advances one beat. Costs are deducted when a ready action starts, then it executes/channels, resolves hooks, recovers and clears timing. Replace/cancel and interrupt exist at engine level; the nearby caller currently uses append and the player projection withholds commands while busy. Continue advances without an action. Target death between queue and resolution, unsupported hooks and actor incapacitation need explicit negative tests in later slices.
- **Effects:** Resolver branches healing before damage and applies statuses afterwards. Block's authored self status and mitigation can be a bounded supported case. Generic non-damage/allied spell or item exposure would be misleading because their upstream owners and multi-effect semantics are unsettled.
- **Outcome/durability:** Both terminal branches clear encounter state and append combat history; nearby admission owns ledger/result. A controlled victory fixture can prove mechanics but is not ordinary selected victory or balance evidence. Test both terminal branches, continued play, exact duplicate/conflict and save/reload without changing difficulty, enemy count, HP or damage.
