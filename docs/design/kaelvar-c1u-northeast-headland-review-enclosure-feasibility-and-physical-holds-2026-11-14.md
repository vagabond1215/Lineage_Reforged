examined the **original mainland-attached northeastern headland** at
original source coastline edges **75–115** (vertices 75–116), its
fragile corridor back toward Verdant, and unresolved source teal/black

**Disposition:** Keep northeastern headland **UNASSIGNED**. The compact
return may be used as the smaller **visual inspection window**, not as

## Why the direct full Verdant extension is rejected

An independent diagnostic formed the naive review shortcut using the
original coast vertex sequence `75..349` and **unchanged** C1-P
Verdant inland return waypoints.

That particular trial forms a valid simple ring in the coordinate
validator, but it still **fails necessary acceptance gates**:

- Its inland return **properly crosses original source coast edge 70**.
- It **overlaps 530 provisional Kharuun component-30 cells**.
- It would therefore violate the selected Kharuun **option B** coast
  and current mutually nonoverlapping C1-P ownership assumptions.
- It does not prove a safe physical Verdant connection.

This corrects an overly broad inference sometimes made from visual
trial previews: a particular extended political ring may fail because
it **crosses source coast or another territory**, even when it is
not itself a self-intersecting polygon. A different proposed
extension must be tested on its own exact geometry.

**No corrected annexation geometry was authored here.**
