## 1. Core distinction: three ways a place can produce or affect beings

| Mechanism | Permitted direction | Not implied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Leyline-generated elemental presence** | A sufficiently exposed, element-specific magical field increases eligibility/probability of ambient phenomena, transient presence, or a properly admitted discrete/persistent **elemental** near the source, including away from dungeons | Guaranteed spawns, always-hostile behavior, instant creature creation, unlimited repeated spawn farming, automatic loot |
| **Dungeon-born monsters/dungeon folk** | Creatures in the user's narrower *dungeon-origin* category originate from an actual dungeon and may later move or be encountered outside it through proven travel and world events | Ordinary wilderness spontaneously creating dungeon monsters, or treating every combat enemy as dungeon-born |
| **Natural beasts and humanoids** | Naturally reproduce and inhabit the ordinary world according to ecology, people/ancestry, and family owners; eligible races/species may also have **dungeon-born counterparts** where a real dungeon origin is certified | Calling every dangerous animal a dungeon monster, fabricating persons from encounter seeds, or removing natural populations because a dungeon exists |

**Important nomenclature bridge:** current `world.monsters` is a broad technical static *enemy-archetype* source. Some records may describe elementals or creature-like combatants, and `world.encounter_templates` / `world.spawn_profiles` can select them outside explicitly authored dungeons. Thus the user's new rule concerns **in-world biological/magical provenance and the narrower category of dungeon-origin monsters/dungeon folk**, not an instruction to retcon every existing `monster.*` record as physically born inside a dungeon. Future classification must explicitly reconcile the two labels. No schema rename or existing spawn deletion is authorized.

An elemental formed at a Fire node remains an elemental manifestation under accepted magical-entity taxonomy even if the combat UI represents it with a `monsterClass` archetype. Similarly, an aggressive wolf stays a naturally occurring beast rather than a dungeon-born monster. A dungeon-born wolf-like creature requires its own actual origin evidence and cannot be silently inferred from the same appearance.

## 2. Elementals near concentrated leyline magic

- A valid near-surface node, including intensified **same-element** intersections, can raise the local *chance* and possible rate/strength of elemental emergence inside its irregular graded influence area. The stronger near-center density can also alter nearby conditions and unusual element-associated weather, following the previous non-linear/diminishing-return and point-source-like falloff direction; no numeric radius, spawn threshold, rate, logarithmic coefficient, or inverse-power formula is selected.
- Keep the accepted stages **ambient effect -> transient coherent presence -> accepted identity-bearing discrete/persistent elemental** distinct. Field strength is an eligibility input, not automatic creation of an actor or instant encounter. Existing elementals may also be reinforced or supported; reusing a source never duplicates their identity, history, loot, or rewards.
- An elemental is not automatically evil, allied, intelligent, aggressive, under religious control, or permanently local. Its temperament, resources, stimulus response, movement and actual combat outcomes remain independently owned.
- **Fire elementals in a forest** are a concrete prospective ecology/hazard conflict: when an actual elemental and flammable habitat coexist and eligible actions/conditions occur, fire spread, damage, evacuation pressure, altered fauna distribution, resource loss and settlement consequences become possible. The forest is not automatically burning merely because the line exists; world fuel/weather, environment/fire and accepted event owners decide what really happens.
- Natural fire, storm, heat, geology, proximity to a shrine and a reported sighting are neither proof of a leyline nor a right to conjure a specific elemental. Rare rupture/breach phenomena, if actually present, may raise exposure but are separate source-certified exceptions.

## 3. Dungeon exposure and theme transformation

A **nearby real dungeon** can be substantially altered by a powerful elemental field, potentially to the point where its creatures, environments, encounter pressures and recognizable theme include that element. This is a *future dungeon-owned, causally sourced transformation*, not an immediate asset reskin or unconditional mutation of all dungeons in the same region.

A future valid causal chain is:
1. Certify dungeon identity and geometry/access, physical proximity or actual magical connection, the specific element and a source-backed exposed influence footprint through time.
2. Establish degree, duration and means of environmental magical penetration: opening, seam, groundwater, portal/breach, dungeon infrastructure or other later owner-approved mechanism. Mere nearby map labels need not guarantee exposure.
3. Determine which existing dungeon habitats, occupants and any origin/creation mechanisms can be affected. Species and monsters must have compatible susceptibility, elemental/variant lineage, or dungeon transformation rules; others may be displaced, harmed or remain unchanged.
4. Admit a dungeon state or historically grounded transition before changing its next encounter composition, hazards, architecture or element-themed presentation. Consequences may be partial, contested, evolving or complete theme changes as the source supports.
5. Preserve lineage: a transformed monster or dungeon occupant needs an original entity/archetype and transformation history, not a duplicate creature or unexplained backfilled origin; previously accepted exploration/combat events, discovered rooms, loot claims, world history, and personal Knowledge must not be rewritten.
6. A dungeon monster may later emerge from its dungeon and threaten nearby land; its *origin* remains the dungeon. Outside encounters then depend on valid access, travel, hazard and spawn authority, not arbitrary wilderness creation.

**No automatic dungeon generator/rewriter:** present `world.monsters` owns static archetypes, `world.encounter_templates` compositions and `world.spawn_profiles` world eligibility descriptors. Dungeon identity, interior state, theme mutation and origin provenance require future named owners; encounter/combat consumers cannot author those facts. No fire-dungeon spawn tables or elementalized variants are implemented by this note.

## 4. Natural beings: magical susceptibility and two distinct eligibility pathways

A naturally occurring beast or humanoid/person may be altered by a leyline **only when it is magically susceptible**, meaning its species/race or individual has a legitimate capacity to receive, wield, or harness such magic under future accepted magic/ancestry authority. Mere presence in a high-affinity landscape does not transform every resident.

In addition to magical susceptibility, user direction requires **at least one** of these independently evidenced pathways:

**A. Innate racial/species elemental interaction.** An authored species-level mechanism allows meaningful strengthening, weakening, affinity or other alteration in the presence of the relevant element. A fire-interacting draconic/reptilian humanoid is an example, not an already confirmed specific ancestry effect, universal protection, combat modifier, or guarantee of enhancement. Elemental compatibility may be beneficial or harmful under its actual physiology/magic.

**B. Sustained exceptionally dense formative exposure.** An eligible developing being is exposed to the potent leyline field around gestation/birth and through **the majority of its formative growth, including gestational and postnatal stages**. The cumulative exposure, high density, source depth, location, susceptibility, developmental stage and survival must all be real. A single visit, brief pregnancy proximity, one spell, a ritual's name, a birth at a shrine or an unverified rumor cannot substitute for the prolonged exposure pathway.

These pathways are about **eligibility to be affected**, not automatic positive alteration. Possible source-approved results include affinity, altered traits or capabilities, debilitation, congenital/developmental disruption, illness, death or no durable change. Actual outcomes, reversibility, heredity and treatment remain open. No guaranteed specially powered offspring, inherited deity trait, new race, spell ownership, stat inflation, heir status or controllability is established.

**Natural versus dungeon-born:** susceptible species can have legitimate naturally occurring populations and independently dungeon-born variants. Dungeon-born provenance is not an automatic magic susceptibility waiver, nor proof that all members of the ancestry were dungeon-created.

## 5. High-density exposure is hazardous; ruptures are exceptional

- **Most leylines have no literal surface rupture or wound.** Ordinary nodes produce graded, depth-sensitive influence without an open magical outflow. A physically accessible exceptionally concentrated focus or an actual magical breach is rare and must be specifically world-authored.
- Proximity to the strongest focus, especially if unusual leakage is present, can be dangerous even to an adult who can use magic: poisonous/overwhelming exposure, physiological harm or permanent changes are plausible. An innate racial protection or exceptionally developed magical competence *may* mediate risk if explicitly supported; competence does not grant blanket immunity.
- Exposure, dose/effective density, duration, barriers, developmental stage, tolerance, protection, healing, elemental compatibility, outcomes, and localized reach remain unquantified. These are future fantasy-world magic, biology/ecology and injury owners, **not real-world toxicology advice or an implemented harm procedure**.
- A high-density near-surface focus should not be commonplace merely because the conceptual element/combinations catalogue is large. Durable body changes require long-term retained causal evidence, not a single numerical affinity tag.

## 6. Plot/quest possibilities: coercive or voluntary seclusion

An institution or cult might believe concentrated magical exposure during gestation and growth could produce an unusually altered child. The user describes two **fictional story premises**:

- **Coercive exploitation:** a cult imprisons a pregnant adult or other person and restricts access to a sufficiently powerful site with the intent of producing a magical being it hopes to control. This is an abuse, captivity and institutional-violence scenario with potential investigation, escape/rescue, prevention, political/legal consequences, and later trauma/survival storylines. The cult's *belief* and goal do not establish actual magical success, authority over the child, or obedience.
- **Voluntary zealotry:** a consenting adult may choose prolonged seclusion near an unusually potent node for faith/ambition. Consent, continued ability to withdraw, environmental risk, other persons' agency, and future family/offspring identity remain material. Adult consent does not mean the future child consented or that the institution controls them.

For either premise, require an actually eligible site and coherent historical era, a real institution/person/quest source, appropriate non-explicit presentation, proof of whether any exposure and transformation actually occurred, and actual owner-accepted consequences. The child is a person, never a produced item, spell component, automatic guild asset, or compulsory thrall. Do not create an executable confinement or child-generation loop, a coercion optimization procedure, or a reward farm from this design note. If encountered in gameplay, the story must preserve character agency and suitable Stakes, law, reputation and Chronicle boundaries.

A cult may incorrectly believe that any strong leyline guarantees a transformed child. Such erroneous theology/science can be a legitimate in-world clue or false claim **only if distinguishable from authoritative world truth**.
