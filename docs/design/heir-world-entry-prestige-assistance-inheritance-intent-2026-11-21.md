# Heir World Entry: Prestige, assistance and inherited circumstances

Date: 2026-11-21
Status: owner design intent; standalone review only. No gameplay or balance implementation.

## Core distinction
Heirs have an independently resolved World Entry catalog, separate from ordinary Core fresh starts and from generic starting bundles. It is the opening situation of an actual existing offspring, not another way to invent a character, spouse, family or estate. The [conception/activation intent](heir-creation-and-activation-design-intent-2026-11-21.md) remains controlling on eligibility: in-game conception evidence, unborn-and-unnamed-only Create Heir, born activation, unlocked Prestige and unused per-progenitor capacity, maturity/lifecycle and actual authority. An independent character can never select an heir entry just by sharing a surname.

## Candidate entry families
Names, unlock keys and relative support are UI concepts, not accepted canonical IDs, tiers, amounts or guaranteed rewards.

| Entry | Circumstances | Potential support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ostracized | Actual exclusion, estrangement or rejection; do not overwrite history to let the player choose this. | Little or none; preserve actual personal possessions and lawful entitlements. |
| Make Your Own Way | Authorized independent departure to acquire experience. | Little or minimal support; no automatic experience bonus or invented kit. |
| A Family Send-Off | Actual family willing and able to support departure; relevant Prestige permission. | Limited approved provisions, travel support, introductions or assistance. |
| Political Marriage | Actual eligible adult, consent, marriage/recognition authority, applicable Prestige unlock and settlement agreement. | Variable lawful dowry/marriage settlement, obligations and connections; never generated spouse or cash from Prestige alone. |
| Estate Successor | Actual estate claim, validated eligibility, relevant Prestige and lawful reconciled assets/liabilities. | Variable inheritance; may be smaller than expected, encumbered or zero. |

Additional circumstances can be authored later: apprenticeships, wards, service, branch establishment, family sponsorship or other source-grounded openings. No fixed support ladder is mandated; poor families, estrangement, legal restrictions and obligations can change any outcome. Prestige unlocks access and permitted assistance/share policies, not a guarantee of resources. Acceptable base options must be explicitly decided; the preview does not impose actual costs or universal unlock tiers. No assumption that all heirs must choose a rich start or that an impoverished start is always available.

## Future authority and orchestration
- Resolve actual offspring/progenitor/World identity and entitlement before selection. Unborn review is provisional; do not assume the future marriage, estate or maturity outcome.
- Derive eligibility from actual family/recognition/marriage, geography, maturity, available assets, source version and relevant Prestige authority. Render disabled reasons; reject forged selections at the owner boundary. Distinguish aid from inheritance, personal property, dowry and liabilities. Respect legal claims and independently existing rights; no automatic deprivation merely for selecting independence.
- For born activation, coordinate applicable authoritative time wait/skip, reevaluate circumstances after elapsed events, run restricted creation and estate/inheritance resolution. Present actual support, claims, obligations and unresolved fields before confirmed publication. Existing shared-world clock and earliest-exit rules remain controlling.
- Actual grants must have a source and owner, be reconciled against estate and other claims, and commit once with the validated character/world/entry/entitlement result. Never duplicate assets or consume the same heir twice. Exact transaction sequencing, cancellation semantics, balance, currency topology, source schema and migration require focused contracts.
- Heir-specific authored opening narrative and real site/arrival conditions must not use ordinary Core starts that contradict established upbringing/history. No generic independent kit-shopping or free starter grants. The production existing creator, Save fields and feature-off flow stay intact until accepted versioned migration.

## Review UI boundary
The eligible unborn and born handoff dialogs show a separate selectable five-concept catalog with explicit sample unlock and factual context gates; support is qualitative only. Dain can inspect Make Your Own Way and A Family Send-Off; Political Marriage is Prestige-unlocked in the fixture but blocked by absent agreement/adult-marriage evidence; Estate Successor is locked. Aldric's unborn offspring can inspect only Make Your Own Way as a provisional idea. Selection changes only the rendered review, not the World, offspring, assets, ledger, age, or Save. Final Create/Activate remains unavailable. Do not export fixture IDs as production IDs.
