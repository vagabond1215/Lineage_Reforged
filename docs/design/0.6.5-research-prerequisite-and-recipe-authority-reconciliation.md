## Recipe And Production-Chain Authority Policy

The integration pass must make the final repo-corrected decision, using the following default posture unless live evidence disproves it:

1. **Recipe records own bounded static transformations.** A recipe records the explicit game-scale inputs, outputs, roles, tools, workplaces, skill requirements, and optional related production-chain identity for one planned transformation.
2. **Production chains own macro process context.** A chain may identify stages, broad inputs, intermediates, outputs, variants, workplaces, and process relationships. A chain reference is descriptive and non-inheriting unless a later schema explicitly says otherwise. Production chains are not automatically authoritative for every recipe's exact batch ratio.
3. **Research informs, but does not directly canonize, quantities.** Research should identify necessary materials, meaningful intermediates, process order, losses, byproducts, and plausible relative scale. Exact recipe integers are authored game-scale batch units selected during repository integration or a later bounded balance decision. They must not be presented as universally exact historical or scientific yields.
4. **Quantity confidence must be explicit.** Classify quantity guidance as source-backed, bounded design inference, balance placeholder, or blocked pending authored input. Reject false precision.
5. **Authority repairs remain narrow.** When a production-chain record is materially wrong or missing a relationship needed by multiple recipes, route a separate narrow chain-authority correction before recipe authoring. Do not broaden `0.6.5` into crafting execution, labor, fuel, time, quality, spoilage, inventory mutation, economy simulation, or magical runtime.
