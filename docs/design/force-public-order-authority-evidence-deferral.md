## 2. Preserved Boundary

- Force owns the future static identity of an enduring organized body whose primary identity is armed defense, civic protection, military service, or organized route protection.
- Watches, militias, garrisons, military forces/commands, and route-security bodies are typed forms within one future family, not separate collections.
- A martial or military order qualifies only when its primary canonical identity is the armed/protective body and religion, guild, institution, faction, business, or another specific owner is not the better authority.
- Affiliation, mandate, coverage, and headquarters are intrinsic relationship concepts.
- A reference-free identity shell is semantically unsafe and remains rejected.
- Current readiness, rosters, ranks, office-holders, assignments, deployment, patrols, schedules, spawning, encounters, combat, AI, arrest, enforcement, cases, access, reputation, runtime, UI, save/account, and gameplay remain outside static force identity.
- Polity, government, jurisdiction, law, court, institution, office, guild, religion/order, faction, business, place/facility, profession/role/title, route-security overlay, quest/reputation, combat, and runtime owners remain separate.

No collection path, prefix contract, schema, validator, live wrapper, focused force test, normal content-lint registration, reference adapter, or consumer exists or is approved.

## 3. Exact Reopening Inputs

Force planning may reopen only when at least one materially new qualifying input exists:

1. **Explicit user-authored or approved force canon**: an exact named force list or seed list that supplies the complete identity and relationship facts in this document.
2. **New canonical repository source**: a durable content or design source intentionally defining one or more enduring armed/protective bodies, their primary identity owner, and the required relationship posture.
3. **Explicitly authorized civic-content authorship pass**: the user intentionally prioritizes force canon and authorizes focused authorship before any schema or JSON implementation.
4. **Concrete ready consumer**: a named consumer with stable target owners proves why one minimal static force contract is required, which relationship semantics it reads, and how validation must fail closed.

A reopening prompt must name the new input, state whether it concerns schema readiness or seed readiness, and map its facts to the minimum gates below. It may still conclude that no schema or candidate is safe.

A ready consumer can justify schema reconsideration only. It cannot create canonical force identity, approve a seed, or authorize content merely because it expects a force id.

## 5. Minimum Schema-Readiness Gate

A future docs-only schema plan may be considered only when the new input proves all of these contract questions without guessing:

1. **Identity boundary**: exact definition of an enduring force body and a fail-closed test separating it from adjacent organizations, places, combat/runtime groups, and overlays.
2. **Family posture**: confirmation that one broad family remains coherent plus controlled form semantics for the required use case.
3. **Affiliation model**: exact relationship kinds, allowed stable target authorities, required/optional cardinality, and explicit posture for independent or unaffiliated forces.
4. **Mandate model**: controlled non-executing descriptive semantics that cannot encode orders, law, arrest power, AI priorities, targets, or current objectives.
5. **Coverage model**: supported target types, cardinality, overlap, temporal posture if any, and explicit distinction from jurisdiction, territorial control, route-security posture, and actual patrol presence.
6. **Headquarters model**: supported place/facility target types, cardinality, and explicit posture for itinerant, distributed, or no-fixed-headquarters forces.
7. **Lifecycle and public posture**: record lifecycle, stable public/protective posture, provenance, and non-implication rules independent of current readiness or deployment.
8. **Validation boundary**: stable dependency owners, fail-closed target resolution, duplicate-identity rules, unknown-field rejection, and forbidden relationship/runtime families.
9. **Consumer justification**: the named consumer or authored input explains why each relationship belongs in the minimal first contract and which fields remain deferred.
10. **No execution**: explicit exclusion of membership/rosters, ranks/office-holders, schedules/patrols, readiness/strength, spawning/encounters/combat, law/enforcement/cases, access/reputation, runtime/UI/save/account, and gameplay.

If any gate remains unresolved, schema planning stays closed. A content-independent schema plan may use hypothetical in-memory fixtures only after these semantics are proven; fixtures never become canon.

## 6. Complete Seed-Readiness Gate

Every future proposed force record must supply all of the following from a qualifying authored source:

1. exact canonical public name;
2. exact intended `force.<slug>` id or explicit authority to derive one unambiguously;
3. proof that the subject is an enduring armed/protective body rather than another organization, a place/facility, a temporary detachment, a combat/runtime group, an overlay, or a projection;
4. supported force form under the approved broad family;
5. non-invented static identity summary;
6. record lifecycle and stable public/protective posture;
7. explicit affiliation relationship or explicitly authored independent/unaffiliated posture using approved target semantics;
8. explicit non-executing mandate posture;
9. explicit coverage relationship or explicitly authored no-fixed-coverage posture using approved target semantics;
10. explicit headquarters relationship or explicitly authored itinerant/distributed/no-fixed-headquarters posture using approved target semantics;
11. durable provenance and notes explaining primary identity ownership and mixed-organization boundaries;
12. explicit non-implication of personnel, ranks, offices, deployment, readiness, patrols, schedules, spawning, encounters, combat, enforcement, access, reputation, runtime, UI, save/account, and gameplay.

All twelve items are mandatory. The candidate must also satisfy the later approved schema. A reviewer must be able to draft the complete record without importing facts from another owner or filling gaps by plausibility.

An approved exact seed list that omits these facts reopens focused authorship/gate review, not JSON implementation.

## 7. Separate Future Gates

### Readiness gate

Apply the schema-readiness or seed-readiness gate only to the named materially new input. Do not rescan unrelated repository surfaces.

### Schema-plan gate

A later documentation pass must decide the exact collection path, prefix, wrapper, fields, controlled vocabularies, relationships, cardinality, target authorities, lifecycle, provenance, forbidden fields, pure validator dependencies, and focused tests. It may still approve no schema.

### Schema/validator gate

Only a later narrow implementation may add schema, pure validator, focused in-memory tests, and schema-file parse coverage. It must not add live content or normal registration.

### Seed-plan gate

Only after the schema foundation exists may a narrow docs-only seed plan apply the complete seed gate and approve exact ids. It may approve zero records.

### Content gate

Only a later explicitly selected implementation may create a live wrapper, and only with records approved by a completed seed plan. Do not create an empty wrapper.

### Registration gate

Normal content-lint registration remains separate until live content exists and passes focused validation. It must prove exact-once wiring.

### Reference gate

Affiliation, coverage, and headquarters references are intrinsic but unauthorized. A schema plan must prove exact semantics, stable target owners, cardinality, dependency-injected validation, lifecycle/validity posture, and non-implication behavior before any reference exists.

### Consumer gate

Government, jurisdiction, law, courts, institutions, offices, guilds, religions/orders, factions, businesses, places, route security, quests, reputation, Knowledge, combat, runtime, UI, and save/account each require later owner-specific integration decisions. Static force identity enables none automatically.

### Runtime gate

Personnel, membership, command, ranks, deployment, readiness, patrols, schedules, spawning, encounters, combat, AI, arrest, enforcement, cases, access, consequences, and simulation remain `0.6+` concerns pending explicit ownership and readiness decisions.

## 8. Owner And Non-Inference Rules

- Mixed organizations have one primary canonical identity owner. Do not duplicate a religion/order, guild, institution, faction, business, or office as a force because it has armed members.
- A place/facility may host a force but cannot create it.
- Route-security posture may later reference or be related to a force, but neither owner creates or overrides the other.
- Jurisdiction and law do not create a force; force identity does not create enforcement power.
- A combat unit, detachment, party, patrol, or encounter group may later reference a force but is not the static force record.
- Current readiness, strength, position, control, patrol presence, and response are runtime/overlay state, not identity facts.
- Missing identity or relationship authority fails closed.
- Do not normalize existing `office.*`, `rep.*`, `authority.*`, route-security, place, combat, or runtime ids into `force.*`.
