For each row, capture account ID/revision/generation, slot ID/generation, campaign/continuity, source and head artifact/publication/revision, receipt/witness IDs, counts and exact pre/post readback where applicable. A row is `PASS` only with its stated new evidence; otherwise record `FAIL`, `BLOCKED` or `UNREACHABLE` and the first exact boundary. An approval rejection cannot be bypassed or counted as proof.
