Its fresh new authority must preserve everything legitimate after cutover. Slice F remains inert historical evidence and is not a required importer for activation.

**2026-09-29 owner-policy supersession:** Backward preservation, migration, reconciliation and export/import of saves from the currently deployed older build are no longer required. At eventual activation, those saves may be invalidated/deleted and players may start a new campaign. This changes the later activation route, not the historical Slice F implementation evidence. ## Decision

**CANONICAL_MATERIALIZATION_CONTRACT_ACCEPTED; ACTIVATION_HELD.** Add a separate, inert IndexedDB version-3 materialization slice before any caller cutover. Slice E's version-2 `legacyCopyRecords` and `legacyCopyManifests` remain immutable source evidence. Version-1 artifact/control/slot/witness stores remain isolated publication evidence, not an importer. No legacy publication is replayed through `CampaignIndexedDbStore.publish`, no identity or provenance is minted, and no source key is changed. A `verified_with_blockers` copy may be represented and diagnosed but cannot become playable authority.

The first executable successor is **DEV-0.7.1 Slice F - Inert Canonical Materialization And Exact Readback**. It only creates generation-scoped canonical records and a readback manifest using injected storage/source, with no `App`, launcher, profile, attempt or save caller import and no activation selection. Activation is a later package, conditional on the fences, repair owners, export/restore and caller conversion below. The current origin cannot prove that an already-open older binary will obey a new marker or lock. An unacknowledged or old-version tab therefore holds activation; a source change after a verified generation causes quarantine, not timestamp selection.

## Version-3 schema and materialization contract

Upgrade `lineage.campaigns` additively from version 2 to 3; retain all six existing stores and records. Add `canonicalRecords` keyed by `[copyId, scopeKind, scopeId, family, identity]` with indexes by generation/scope and generation/scope/family, and `canonicalManifests` keyed by `[copyId, scopeKind, scopeId]`. `scopeKind` is `account` with the exact account ID or `origin_unscoped` with empty `scopeId`; obsolete keys never receive a guessed account. This avoids collision with a real account ID. Every record has record version 1, original key, exact raw JavaScript string, Slice-E digest, parsed version when validated, exact identity fields derived from the key and owner validator, validation posture, and named finding codes. The original key is unique within a generation; ambiguous identities or collisions block readiness. The canonical projection is a queryable index over retained raw truth, not a replacement for the raw. Use complete key comparison, including dots in IDs, and the same UTF-16LE digest rule as Slice E.

| --- | --- | --- |
| `artifact` | Account/artifact ID, complete v7 envelope raw and snapshot; **every** accepted non-head and fork artifact | Envelope, snapshot, campaign, publication and account identity; never derive lineage from array order or replay publication. |
| `slot` | Account/slot ID, exact v7 address raw even for non-head or closed campaigns | Referenced artifact bytes and campaign control, account-wide slot contention. |
| `control` | Account/campaign ID and exact v1 control | Head/previous-head identity and revision against retained artifacts; no head chosen by timestamp. |

| `migration_receipt`, `migration_source`, `v6_slot` | Exact legacy character/source/slot identities and raw v1 receipt or v6 envelope | Stable source group and receipt/target relationships; pending, malformed or unverifiable groups block. Do not remigrate or generate IDs. |

Launcher session, credential and UI-preference keys remain launcher-owned `localStorage`, outside these records and export. A separate `authoritySelections` store keyed by account ID is reserved for a later schema/activation package; Slice F does not create or write a selection. A manifest records schema/validator version, source `copyId` and digest, exact source-key/digest set, account scope, canonical record count and digest, per-family counts, graph findings, existing-v1 overlap, and `provisional`, `verified`, or `blocked` status. One account/origin scope is committed in one IndexedDB read-write transaction; a transaction abort leaves no complete new manifest. Close/reopen and independently enumerate every canonical record, original raw/digest and source manifest before changing a manifest to `verified` or `blocked`; restart and repeat exact readback before any later activation. A same-source retry is idempotent. A changed source gets a new generation and leaves the old one inert. Never overwrite another generation or collapse distinct raw records into one identity.

Slice F reads a complete version-2 staged generation and verifies its manifest/record digests before deriving version-3 records; the injected `Storage` is only for re-enumeration and mismatch detection, never for mutating legacy readers. Slice E's findings are an input, not final semantic authority. Owner validators must independently re-evaluate raw bytes and the full account graph. A formerly blocked generation can only become `verified` when a named repair owner has produced a new stable source generation and every finding is resolved, or a pure validator proves the prior conservative finding was false without changing raw truth. The latter requires a recorded validator version and independent test. A `blocked` canonical manifest remains exportable/quarantined and has no activation path. Existing v1 records with overlapping identities are compared byte-for-byte and semantically; mismatch is a blocker, never silently merged.

## Activation and caller ownership

Later activation needs an account-scoped authority selection in the **same IndexedDB transaction** that verifies the selected canonical generation/complete account graph and excludes competing v1 state. Before that commit: finish all pending publication/consumer/attempt/migration repairs through their existing owner or a separately implemented async equivalent; re-enumerate the entire origin source against the staged key/raw generation; complete exact close/reopen and browser-restart readback; and prove export/restore. The selection includes account ID, generation, source digest, schema/validator version and a monotonic authority epoch. There is exactly one selected store per account. The old source stays immutable and readable during initial activation. Source and selected store divergence becomes a named quarantine; neither timestamp nor current active-account pointer decides it.

All live campaign/account operations must route through one async account repository that reads selection before **every** write and compares the selected epoch/head in the same IndexedDB transaction. For a legacy account, the repository may use the old owner only while no selection exists and the old-writer fence allows it. An IDB-selected account must never fall back to legacy writes on unavailable/blocked/quota errors. New tabs signal epoch changes (for example through `BroadcastChannel`/storage events) for prompt UI invalidation, but signals are advisory; transaction-time checks are authority. A stale IDB tab fails closed with `stale_head`/`stale_epoch`, reloads exact current state and asks the user to retry. A stale legacy tab must be unable to write, or activation remains held. `navigator.locks`, a localStorage sentinel, and an IDB marker cannot bind old code that never checks them. Therefore a two-stage deployed writer-fence protocol and a demonstrable no-unacknowledged-writer condition are mandatory before activation. Merely asking users to close tabs is insufficient proof. If that condition cannot be established on this origin, preserve inert materialization and do not activate.

Account reset/delete are **not** simple database clearing: write an epoch/tombstone first, stop all account writes, make the requested state transition idempotent across localStorage auth and IndexedDB, retain a recovery/export point until completion, then report success. A crash between stores resumes the tombstone operation. Old copies must not resurrect a reset/deleted account. Credentials and launcher session are deleted only by the launcher owner after the storage transition is durable; malformed/partial state blocks play rather than guessing. This is a later separate package, not Slice F.

## Repair, rollback, failure and acceptance gates

Blocked source has a production-reachable owner: existing publication recovery and App consumer repair for pending publications/receipts; attempt coordinator for same-source attempts; legacy migration owner for v6 receipt/source groups; Soundings provenance owner for missing/conflicting first witness/artifact; account profile owner for nested/history links; launcher/account repair for active pointer, reset/delete and unscoped/unknown ownership. Each must expose a user-visible pending/error/repair route, preserve original bytes and verify exact post-repair generation. Malformed or unowned evidence remains quarantined and blocks that scope; it is never auto-deleted. Cross-account/origin blockers can hold all activation if ownership cannot be safely localized.

Dynamic `navigator.storage.estimate()` is advisory, not a size guarantee. Quota, transaction abort, blocked upgrade, unavailable/evicted IDB or digest/readback failure leaves selection unchanged and prior legacy authority loadable. Do not delete history to free capacity. Before any selection, test full user-controlled export and restore of every canonical record, manifest, profile, receipt, first witness/artifact and quarantine evidence. Pre-first-IDB-write rollback may return to an exact verified unchanged legacy generation; after IDB writes, a blind flip to stale legacy bytes is forbidden and requires a full export/restore or explicit reconciliation. Retire old keys only in a distinct approved cleanup after active-store rollback and export acceptance, with launcher credentials separately preserved.

Package order: **F** inert version-3 materialization/exact readback with synthetic injected source; **G** old-writer fence and async account repository plus real launcher/App/runLifecycle callers, reset/delete and reachable repair flows, still with no activation until proved; **H** export/restore and account-scoped activation/rollback with source recheck, epoch and quarantine; **I** independent two-browser and long-run acceptance. G is the earliest package allowed to change live caller behavior: async pending/error flow and fenced legacy operations only, while `localStorage` remains the selected campaign authority. H is the earliest package allowed to switch live reads/writes to IndexedDB, after G and its gates pass. If G cannot prove the old-client fence, stop at inert F and install a dedicated fence decision before H. F's rollback point is simply the unchanged legacy source, version-2 stage and live callers. Do not combine F with G/H or presume that a nominally small helper test accepts them.

Slice F tests: all families and exact UTF-16 raw/digest preservation; multi-account and unscoped quarantine; non-head/fork artifacts and every slot; applied and missing-first Soundings witness; v6 receipt/source, pending attempts/recoveries and account consumers; existing-v1 overlap; source mutation, same-source retry, different generation, crash/provisional resume, injected quota/abort, blocked upgrade/unavailable, close/reopen exact readback and no live imports or source writes. Later G/H native caller tests must cover old and new tabs, stale IDB epoch/head, source change before selection, account reset/delete crash points, competing slot recovery, failed activation, and export/restore. Run on Chromium **and Firefox**, then the 1,000-action/save plus second-campaign/non-head/Soundings workload in both; report actual browser storage estimates and exact serialized bytes. This is an engineering workload, not a product save-count cap. Slice C's 5 MiB failure, combat admission and parent acceptance remain held until these later gates and an ordinary combat workload pass.
