## 2. Preserved Boundary

- Government owns a governing arrangement or organization, separate from polity identity, institution, office, force, settlement administration, rulers/people, and runtime.
- Jurisdiction owns applicability, separate from physical place, polity, claim/control, government organization, law, courts, force coverage, property legal labels, access, citizenship/legal standing, and runtime.
- Government is inherently linked to a governed authority and temporal posture.
- Jurisdiction is inherently linked to a competent authority and explicit scope.
- No reference-free schema is coherent for either owner.
- No referenced schema is approved until its target types, cardinality, validity, and overlap semantics are evidence-backed.
- Law remains downstream of jurisdiction. Force/public order and enforcement remain separate.

## 3. Exact Reopening Inputs

Government or jurisdiction planning may reopen only when at least one materially new qualifying input exists:

1. **Explicit user-authored or approved canon**: an exact government and/or jurisdiction list that supplies canonical identity, governed/competent authority, scope where applicable, cardinality, lifecycle, temporal validity, provenance, and non-implication facts.
2. **New canonical repository source**: a durable content or design source intentionally defining a governing arrangement or jurisdiction applicability record with the required relationship and temporal facts.
3. **Explicitly authorized civic-content authorship pass**: the user intentionally prioritizes civic canon and authorizes focused authorship before schema or JSON implementation.
4. **Concrete ready consumer**: a named consumer with stable referenced owners proves one minimal static relationship contract, exact semantics, and fail-closed validation need. Consumer demand qualifies only when it supplies contract evidence; a hook or string alone does not.

A reopening prompt must name the new input, identify which owner it concerns, and map each supplied fact to the missing relationship/cardinality/temporal gate. It may still conclude that no schema or candidate is safe.
