## 5. Stabilization, Suppression, Definitive Care, Resolution, And Restoration

- **stabilization**: an owner accepts that the named process is bounded or controlled under stated current conditions; it may still be active;
- **temporary suppression**: an owner accepts that expression or progression is constrained while a capability, material, environment, or other condition holds;
- **supportive care**: an accepted care result supports body function, comfort, access, or safe continuation without claiming source control;
- **definitive treatment**: an applicable owner accepts an intervention aimed at causal control or repair that immediate stabilization cannot supply;
- **process resolution**: the process owner accepts that the named process is no longer active;
- **functional recovery**: the functional-state owner accepts restored capability from current owner-certified facts;
- **anatomical restoration**: the injury/body/restoration owners accept repair or recreation beyond ordinary stabilization and healing;
- **resurrection**: death and magic owners accept restoration from actual death while eligibility remains open under Stakes.

Stabilization does not imply consciousness, mobility, survival, full recovery, process resolution, definitive care, anatomical restoration, resurrection, or immunity to recurrence.

Two narrow low-severity cases may move directly to resolution only after their own owner accepts complete cause removal and normal function: an uncomplicated obstruction that is fully removed, and a genuinely superficial bounded burn whose injury owner later accepts ordinary resolution. These exceptions do not create a generic “first aid equals cured” rule.

## 6. Transport, Delay, Monitoring, And Reassessment

The following are accepted qualitative reassessment triggers for later contract work:

- movement, extraction, or a materially different transport posture;
- material delay under the process owner’s policy;
- environmental change, including exposure, shelter, weather, water, heat, or cold;
- new, returning, or observably worsening symptoms;
- failed, partial, expired, or condition-dependent intervention response;
- changed destination capability or access;
- changed provider, equipment, supply, contamination, shelter, route, vehicle, escort, or helper capability;
- an accepted upstream injury, body, process, hazard, magic, or functional-state transition.

Each trigger creates a reassessment opportunity only through the owning contract. It does not create an automatic penalty, a global check, or an omniscient hidden-state update. Exact intervals, travel modifiers, probabilities, and deterioration curves remain prohibited.

Transport and institutions consume accepted process, functional, care, route, and capability facts. They never diagnose or mutate a process merely by moving the patient or accepting arrival.
