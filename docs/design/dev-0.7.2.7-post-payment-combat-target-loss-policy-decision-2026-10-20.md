# DEV-0.7.2.7 Post-Payment Combat Target-Loss Policy Decision

**POST_PAYMENT_TARGET_LOSS_POLICY_ACCEPTED_FOR_CURRENT_ATTACKS; NO_RUNTIME_REPAIR_REQUIRED; GAME_VERSION_NOT_READY.** This support suffix of DEV-0.7.2 uses clean synchronized `master`/`origin/master` `d1495d0c27185139f4cad866371332f05e8f7801`, Game `0.1.1-prealpha`, playability `INTEGRATED_LOOP`; development impact `supports_current_band`, Game-version impact `none`. It decides the narrow policy left open by [DEV-0.7.2.6](dev-0.7.2.6-queued-combat-target-legality-and-payment-record-2026-10-20.md). Scope is the current single-target basic melee, equipped Arming Sword `combat.melee.primary`, and equipped Short Bow `combat.ranged.primary` enemy attacks. No balance, content, schema, runtime, test, selected UI or Game-version change is authorized by this decision.

## Evidence and alternatives

The nearby owner admits an exact action/target command against a source snapshot, then queues it and advances a beat. The combat owner checks target and resources immediately before starting; payment occurs at start, and the action records start, resolution and recovery ticks. At resolution it filters invalid enemy targets, then enters recovery; weapon skill-gain candidate creation requires a valid enemy target at resolution start. The nearby ledger's `active` result records an admitted step, not a successful hit or effect. These are current source facts, not a prior refund policy. Controlled DEV-0.7.2.6 owner/caller tests prove spent cost, no effect and full recovery after target incapacitation, as well as cancellation without payment when target loss precedes start. Fresh focused combat/nearby/equipment/persistence tests pass **88/88**. No ordinary selected target-loss playthrough or full browser restart is claimed.

| Choice | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refund after payment | **No refund.** The committed HP/MP/Stamina cost remains paid. | Starting the action is the cost boundary. Reversing it after later world changes would require a new refund owner and replay rule; none exists. |
| Recovery after all targets become invalid | **Keep the recorded full recovery.** The started action progresses through `recovering` to `resolved`. | The actor committed the execution interval; a shortened or free retry would change timing and action economy without an authored rule. |
| Alternative target | **No automatic retarget.** Never transfer effects to another combatant merely because one is available. | Caller authority and the retained command name an exact target ID. A silent target substitution would change the accepted choice. |
| Effect and skill gain | **No enemy damage/status/interrupt effect and no weapon skill-gain candidate or cap consumption when the target is invalid at resolution.** | Target legality is rechecked at effect time; a spent action alone is not a successful attack against an eligible enemy. |
| Receipt and result wording | **Keep the one accepted nearby command receipt and current encounter result.** `resolved` means the action finished its lifecycle, not that it hit. | Admission, progression and effect are distinct facts. Do not add a synthetic hit/miss receipt or claim damage from `active`/`resolved`. |

Target loss here means the selected enemy is defeated, incapacitated, absent from the encounter, or no longer on the opposing team after payment but before resolution. The same rule applies when another legal enemy remains; that enemy is unaffected. Before payment, the DEV-0.7.2.6 rule still cancels an enemy action with no valid target without cost. This decision does not generalize to future multi-target actions, healing, Block, spells, consumables, actor defeat, interruption, surrender or equipment/ammunition ownership; each retains its existing separate owner gate.
