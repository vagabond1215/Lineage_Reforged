## Structural target contract

**Owner and units.** One player body-state/metabolism owner receives an accepted food receipt and time-advancement facts. Use **normalized energy units (NEU)** for food energy, metabolic expenditure, short-term signed balance and stored-body-fat energy equivalent. The existing authored `calories` and `dailyCalories: 100` provide the provisional NEU scale: an ordinary Human's current daily target is 100 NEU, not 100 literal kcal. Keep the existing authored `protein`, `carbs` and `fat` as distinct **normalized nutrient quantities (NNQ)** until a conversion is accepted. NNQ are amounts, never percentages or independent daily quotas. A later numeric calibration may revise the 100-NEU reference only through a deliberate content/balance and save decision.

**Human and lineages.** Human is the common `1.0` metabolic reference. Existing Human grain/protein/fruit food-tag biases remain explicit food-handling modifiers, not a second global metabolic baseline. One shared calculation applies the currently authored small lineage coefficients for calorie utilization, protein recovery, carbohydrate handling, dietary-fat handling and hydration exactly once at a documented stage. Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Gnome, Halfling and other playable profiles remain comparative inputs; their HP, MP, Intelligence, class reputation and magical affinity do not imply additional caloric demand. Do not replace or double-apply existing coefficients while changing field semantics. Fantasy lineage variation needs equal-input Human/Elf/Orc and other contrasting checks before acceptance.

These are current source values from `player-origins.ts`, not newly accepted reserve release or expenditure coefficients. Other playable lineages retain their existing authored profiles and the same single-owner rule.

**Distinct state.** The future owner needs: (1) an accepted intake receipt linked to the depleted inventory source; (2) a bounded pending-digestion quantity with stable receipt identity and remaining release; (3) nonnegative available Carbs, dietary Fat and Protein NNQ pools; (4) a signed zero-centered *rolling net food-energy supply minus demand* in NEU as a derived observation, not a fourth spendable pool; (5) a separate nonnegative long-term stored-body-fat NEU-equivalent; (6) hydration and existing fatigue/recovery state. Carbs and dietary Fat are short-term substrates; Protein is available tissue-maintenance/recovery material and may also be allocated to energy only through one explicit owner rule. Stored body fat is not dietary Fat and cannot directly refill Stamina. Each pool needs an accepted physical capacity or display denominator; until then no real three-bar UI is truthful.

**One-credit accounting.** Each admitted food amount has one authoritative `calories` total. Macro data describe its nutrient distribution and constraints; they never add a second calorie grant. At each deterministic digestion step, allocate no more than the unreleased share of the food's single NEU total and unreleased shares of its three NNQ amounts. The owner must conserve `accepted intake = pending + absorbed + explicitly documented conversion loss` for each dimension, with caps/overflow recorded rather than silently credited. If macro quantities become fuel, the owner must choose one accepted conversion/allocation rule, debit the corresponding available pool and prevent the same material from also supporting full protein maintenance or another energy use. A positive signed balance is not itself additional energy: only an actual unspent surplus ledger can transfer energy into body fat after accepted elapsed time. A negative balance may coexist with stored fat because mobilization is rate-limited; any fat draw is separately debited and may only cover an actual uncovered metabolic demand.

**Time and demand.** Consumption admits a receipt at the selected tick, not instant usable nutrients or Stamina. A shared tick advancement releases a bounded amount, charges positive basal expenditure even during sleep, adds the activity owner's already admitted physical demand once, and applies recovery/protein and reserve rules. Ordinary travel/work and intense exertion can differ by authored intensity, with carbohydrate availability relevant to burst demand. Daily rollover resets *daily reporting counters only*; it must not clear pending digestion, available nutrient pools, body fat or the rolling signed balance. A deterministic same-tick duplicate must neither consume another item nor release nutrients again. Rest meal support cannot become free physical food: it must be tied to an accepted food source/receipt or remain a separately labeled legacy support effect during migration. Combat needs a deliberate time/caller integration, not private calorie arithmetic.

**Physiological and gameplay boundaries.** Available protein, adequate energy, qualifying physical stimulus and elapsed recovery jointly gate maintenance/adaptation. An empty protein pool does not instantly lower Strength; one missed meal or one empty carb/fat pool cannot cause structural atrophy. Mild sustained deficits may draw body fat at a bounded rate; sustained severe uncovered deficits and protein shortage first affect fatigue/recovery, then prolonged body condition under separately accepted thresholds. Base attributes remain immutable. Hydration stays a separate water state. Magic-specific metabolic demand remains a future owner decision. The [FAO/WHO/UNU energy-requirements principles](https://www.fao.org/4/y5686e/y5686e04.htm) support only the relative reference: energy intake and expenditure balance over time; basal function continues at rest; activity and food processing add expenditure. They do not set game values, lineage biology or per-tick coefficients.

## Exact implementation hold and migration gate

This structural contract is **not yet a numerical/save contract**. The following choices are required together before even one live reserve can be added safely:

1. **Calorie and macro conversion:** define whether the authored `calories` value is the sole food-energy authority under every food-tag/lineage modifier; select macro allocation/conversion weights and rounding so effective NEU and NNQ remain reconcilable for all nine current foods, partial servings and future meals. The existing immediate `effectiveCarbs + effectiveCalories × gain` formula is not conserved.
2. **Human demand and time calibration:** specify basal NEU per tick, sleep factor, activity intensity integration, digestion/release class and conversion cost, rolling-balance horizon, three NNQ pool capacities and no-double-credit overflow treatment. Current 100 NEU/day is only a scale anchor, not a chosen BMR or sleep cost.
3. **Reserve and protein policy:** specify fuel-use ordering/substitution, protein maintenance debit, fat-storage and fat-mobilization rate/efficiency, surplus buffer, deficit consequence windows and difficulty modifiers. The accepted decisions explicitly leave these coefficients open; research candidates are not automatically canon.
4. **Save compatibility:** decide versioned persistent shape, old-save migration/defaults, one-time mapping or deliberate non-mapping of `energyReserve.quick/stored`, rollover `energyBalance` and `proteinSufficiency`, pending meal identity, restart/replay, malformed state and retained legacy readers. Current saves cannot reveal historical macro availability or true body fat. Do not seed plausible-looking pools from legacy gauges and present them as observed truth.
5. **Caller authority:** inventory every rest/travel/activity/exploration/combat tick and consume path, define exactly-once food and time receipts, and resolve whether rest `mealSupport` is consumed stock, abstract provisioning or a legacy transition. Only the shared body-state owner may perform metabolic math.

**No code, schema, food, difficulty, UI or save edit is made here.** A small runtime slice would otherwise produce a parallel or partial metabolism with unverifiable old saves. The next bounded task is a **Normalized Metabolic Units, Food Conversion And Save Migration Decision**, using all nine food profiles, actual caller receipts and fixed-point conservation examples; it must select the missing numerical and compatibility rules before a red-test owner implementation. This decision stays outside the installed DEV-0.7.2.13 combat route.
