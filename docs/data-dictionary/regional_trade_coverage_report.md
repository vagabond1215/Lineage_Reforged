# Regional Trade Coverage Report

Scope: this report evaluates the major inhabited trade regions in the first authored world map: the five continents plus the four named island systems.

Coverage bands:

- `surplus`: dependable export-scale overproduction
- `strong`: self-sufficient with routine export headroom
- `moderate`: locally viable but not comfortably export-heavy
- `limited`: unstable or narrow category coverage
- `scarce`: structurally import-biased
- `none`: effectively absent as a domestic base

## Kaelvar

- Climate and biome baseline: dry-year Mediterranean woodland, scrub, steppe, and upland meadow.
- Coverage: staples `moderate`, herd/game `strong`, maritime `moderate`, timber/fiber `moderate`, metals/stone `strong`, herbs/reagents `moderate`, luxury goods `limited`.
- Domestic strengths: wine grapes, wool, goats, horse stock, copper, iron, salt, and coastal fisheries.
- Main gaps: dependable bulk grain, silk, tropical luxuries, rare reagents, and exotic hardwoods.
- Likely trade partners: Draemor for grain; Serathyl for warm-climate finished goods and seaborne craft exchange; Talmyra for spices and exotic imports.

## Valtherion

- Climate and biome baseline: standard temperate river basins, mixed forest, deciduous forest, and steppe.
- Coverage: staples `surplus`, herd/game `strong`, maritime `moderate`, timber/fiber `strong`, metals/stone `strong`, herbs/reagents `moderate`, luxury goods `limited`.
- Domestic strengths: grain, horses, cattle, sheep, orchards, flax, timber, and mountain-fed metals.
- Main gaps: spices, tropical crops, rare hardwoods, exotic animals, pearls, and prestige luxuries.
- Likely trade partners: Talmyra for spices and tropical goods; Serathyl for maritime luxuries and citrus; Myridian Chain for pearls and sea luxuries.

## Serathyl

- Climate and biome baseline: mild-winter maritime forests, rainforests, coasts, and orchard-friendly uplands.
- Coverage: staples `limited`, herd/game `moderate`, maritime `strong`, timber/fiber `strong`, metals/stone `limited`, herbs/reagents `strong`, luxury goods `strong`.
- Domestic strengths: hardwood lumber, ship timber, fish, shellfish, wine, citrus, and dye or aromatic plants.
- Main gaps: bulk grain, iron, heavy building stone, and large horse herds.
- Likely trade partners: Valtherion for metal and industrial goods; Draemor for grain; Kaelvar for wool and upland metals.

## Draemor

- Climate and biome baseline: wet-year basin agriculture, prairie, deciduous forest, marsh, and fen.
- Coverage: staples `surplus`, herd/game `strong`, maritime `moderate`, timber/fiber `strong`, metals/stone `limited`, herbs/reagents `moderate`, luxury goods `limited`.
- Domestic strengths: grain, livestock, horses, leather, wool, flax, and freshwater protein.
- Main gaps: precious metals, tropical luxuries, silk, rare woods, and prestige imports.
- Likely trade partners: Valtherion for industrial and scholarly exchange; Talmyra for tropical luxuries; Serathyl for maritime crafts, dyes, and specialty woods.

## Talmyra

- Climate and biome baseline: hot-year tropical rainforest, seasonal forest, cloud forest, savanna, and mangrove coasts.
- Coverage: staples `moderate`, herd/game `moderate`, maritime `strong`, timber/fiber `moderate`, metals/stone `moderate`, herbs/reagents `strong`, luxury goods `surplus`.
- Domestic strengths: sugarcane, cacao, coffee, tea, tropical fruit, aromatics, rare hardwoods, and exotic animal products.
- Main gaps: cool-weather grain reliability, wool or linen breadth, dense draft-animal capacity, and finished steel.
- Likely trade partners: Draemor and Valtherion for staple security and metal goods; Serathyl for maritime craft exchange and luxury redistribution.

## Myridian Chain

- Climate and biome baseline: mild northern archipelago ecology centered on marine production with limited island forestry.
- Coverage: staples `scarce`, herd/game `scarce`, maritime `surplus`, timber/fiber `limited`, metals/stone `scarce`, herbs/reagents `limited`, luxury goods `moderate`.
- Domestic strengths: fish, shellfish, preserved marine foods, pearls, and naval resupply geography.
- Main gaps: grain, livestock, cloth, heavy timber, and most metal goods.
- Likely trade partners: Kaelvar and Valtherion for food, cloth, and manufactured goods; Draemor for bulk grain.

## Lantern Isles

- Climate and biome baseline: long-summer warm islands with marine production and only narrow agricultural pockets.
- Coverage: staples `scarce`, herd/game `none`, maritime `strong`, timber/fiber `limited`, metals/stone `scarce`, herbs/reagents `moderate`, luxury goods `moderate`.
- Domestic strengths: shellfish, pelagic fish, tea or citrus niche harvests, and relay-port geography.
- Main gaps: grain, herd animals, cloth, metal goods, and large construction timber.
- Likely trade partners: Serathyl for ships and timber; Talmyra for warm-climate food and aromatics; Valtherion for hard staples and finished goods.

## Serpent's Wake

- Climate and biome baseline: unstable-year storm arc with mangroves, tropical dry forest pockets, and strong marine access.
- Coverage: staples `scarce`, herd/game `none`, maritime `strong`, timber/fiber `limited`, metals/stone `scarce`, herbs/reagents `moderate`, luxury goods `strong`.
- Domestic strengths: warm-sea fish, shellfish, sugar, select tropical crops, whale products, and storm-cast salvage.
- Main gaps: stable staple grain, herd animals, cloth, finished metals, and dependable internal provisioning.
- Likely trade partners: Talmyra for climate-compatible exchange; Draemor for staples; Serathyl for ships, tools, and preservation goods.

## Dawnreach Isles

- Climate and biome baseline: cold-island marine ecology backed by taiga, conifer forest, meadow, and tundra margins.
- Coverage: staples `scarce`, herd/game `none`, maritime `surplus`, timber/fiber `limited`, metals/stone `limited`, herbs/reagents `scarce`, luxury goods `moderate`.
- Domestic strengths: cold-water fish, whale routes, marine oils, seal or walrus products, and remote wayfinding positions.
- Main gaps: staple grain, fruit, textiles, herd stock, and broad plant diversity.
- Likely trade partners: Valtherion for staples and finished goods; Myridian Chain for northern-sea redistribution; Kaelvar for wool and upland trade goods.

## Trade Pattern Summary

- Grain powerhouses: Valtherion and Draemor.
- Warm-climate luxury producers: Talmyra, Serathyl, and secondarily the southern island systems.
- Marine specialists: Myridian Chain, Serpent's Wake, Dawnreach Isles, and Lantern Isles.
- Metal and upland suppliers: Valtherion and Kaelvar.
- Regions structurally dependent on trade for staples: Myridian Chain, Lantern Isles, Serpent's Wake, and Dawnreach Isles.
- Regions structurally dependent on trade for luxuries: Kaelvar, Draemor, and Valtherion.
